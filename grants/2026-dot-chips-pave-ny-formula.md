## Summary

**Formula aid, not a grant.** Filed so the harness knows it exists and
does not re-discover it as an "opportunity". CHIPS (Consolidated Local
Street and Highway Improvement Program) apportions state money to every
town by lane-miles in NYSDOT's Local Highway Inventory; PAVE-NY and
Extreme Winter Recovery are additional formula streams paid on the same
reimbursement schedule. Eligible uses: resurfacing, bridge and culvert
work, sidewalks in LHI right-of-way, certain equipment with NYSDOT
approval. Towns may roll apportionments forward to save for larger
projects. Source: NY Association of Towns "CHIPS Facts and Resource
Guide"
(https://nytowns.org/common/Uploaded%20files/CHIPS%20Facts%20and%20Resource%20Guide%20with%20Contacts.pdf,
read 2026-11-04); the NYSDOT CHIPS page itself was not fetched.

## Eligibility

Every NY town with lane-miles in the LHI receives an apportionment;
there is no application. Towns receive "28.1% of the 58.6% municipality
pool, split by each town's lane-miles on file with NYSDOT."

**Verdict: Town of Kingston already receives this** (presumably through
the highway department — confirm with the supervisor/highway
superintendent).

## Fit

Not an opportunity to pursue; relevant as (a) the baseline the highway
budget already relies on and (b) a possible local-match source for
culvert/road grants (WQIP, BRIDGE NY, Tributary Restoration) where state
funds are allowed as match — check each program's match rules (WQIP and
Hudson Estuary grants disallow other NYS funds as match).

## Effort

- Reimbursement via Form CP73 within 18 months of expenditure; SFY
  2026-27 cut-offs 6/12/26, 8/4/26, 10/26/26, 2/5/27; quarterly OSC
  payments.
- Keep the Local Highway Inventory current — apportionment depends on
  it.
- Projects over $35,000 must be competitively bid under GML.
