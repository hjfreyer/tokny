## Summary

Small predevelopment grants for the planning that precedes a water or
waste disposal project in a financially distressed rural community:
feasibility studies that support an application for water/waste
project funding, preliminary design and engineering analysis, and
technical assistance and environmental impact analysis to develop
applications for financial assistance. The planning must relate to a
project that "builds, enlarges, extends, or improves rural water,"
wastewater, solid waste, or storm water facilities. The fact sheet
(https://www.rd.usda.gov/media/file/download/rus-wep-search-fs-07292026.pdf,
updated July 2026) confirms there is no deadline; applications are
accepted year-round through the local RD office.

## Eligibility

From the program page (read 2026-11-04): "Most state and local
governmental entities," nonprofits, and federally recognized tribes may
apply. Eligible areas: "Rural areas with a population of 2,500 or
less" that "Have a median household income below the poverty line" or
"less than 80 percent of the statewide non-metropolitan median
household income," per latest Census data.

**Verdict:** The Town of Kingston qualifies on applicant type and
population (~900 residents). The income test — town MHI below the
poverty line or below 80% of NY's nonmetro median — needs
confirmation from Census data; if the town's income is above that
threshold it is not eligible.

## Fit

A natural first step if the town is considering any water, sewer, or
storm water project but has no engineering report; it would pay for
the study that an RD Water & Waste Disposal application requires.
Whether such a project is contemplated needs confirmation.

## Effort

Program page: contact a program official in the state before
completing forms; guidance, forms and certifications are via RD Apply.
Governing regulation is 7 CFR Part 1774. Expect a SAM.gov registration
and a board resolution authorizing the application.
