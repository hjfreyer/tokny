## Summary

NYSERDA shares the cost of an "objective, site-specific, and targeted"
energy study for a facility: energy efficiency analyses, deep energy
savings, clean heating and cooling (heat pumps), distributed energy
resources (solar, storage), and long-term energy plans. Open PON 4192
with an online application portal; the page gives no closing date. The
study is the usual first step before a municipal building retrofit or
heat-pump project (and before NYSERDA/utility incentives).

## Eligibility

Program page (read 2026-11-04): "Commercial, industrial, institutional,
and multifamily facilities, including small businesses and
not-for-profits, that pay into the electric System Benefits Charge
(SBC)." Municipalities and local governments are not named on the page;
a town hall or highway garage on a Central Hudson commercial account is
an institutional facility paying the SBC, but that reading needs
confirmation with NYSERDA (email on the page) or in the PON 4192
addendum
(https://www.nyserda.ny.gov/-/media/Project/Nyserda/Files/Programs/FlexTech/FlexTech-PON-4192-Addendum.pdf,
not read).

**Verdict: probably eligible as an institutional SBC-paying customer;
needs confirmation.**

## Fit

A town hall or highway garage energy study (lighting, heating, envelope,
heat pump feasibility) is cheap and sets up later capital grants (e.g.
Climate Smart Communities renewable energy at municipal facilities).
Whether the town's buildings are on SBC-paying accounts needs
confirmation.

## Effort

- Review PON 4192 steps; submit through the FlexTech application
  portal; pick a consultant from NYSERDA's list or use your own.
- Scope of work and budget templates on the FlexTech documents page.
- A board resolution is likely needed to contract for the study.
