## Summary

EPF grants to prepare or update a Local Waterfront Revitalization
Program (including climate-resilience updates), prepare an LWRP
component such as a Watershed Management Plan, or implement an approved
LWRP (design/construction: trails, public access, fishing access,
living shorelines, stormwater green infrastructure). 2026-27 round (RFA
#26-LWRP-17) due 4:00 pm, July 31, 2026 via the CFA; expected again in
the 2027 CFA.

## Eligibility

RFA (read 2026-11-04): "A village, town, or city located along New
York's coasts or inland waterways" as designated under Executive Law
Article 42 (list at Executive Law §911 /
https://dos.ny.gov/inland-waterway). Counties and regional planning
councils may apply on behalf of eligible municipalities with consent.
Not-for-profits are ineligible as applicants.

DOS's designated inland waterway list (read 2026-11-04) includes, in
Ulster County: "Esopus Creek (Upper and Lower Branches)", "Rondout
Creek", "Wallkill River" and "Sawkill Creek (Ulster County)". The
Ashokan Reservoir is not listed.

**Verdict: eligible only if the Town of Kingston lies "along" one of
those designated waterways.** The Sawkill Creek is the likeliest
candidate; whether it runs through the town, and whether DOS treats the
town as "located along" it, needs confirmation (ask LWRP@dos.ny.gov).

## Fit

If eligible, a Watershed Management Plan component or a small public
access/trail project on the Sawkill could fit; planning projects require
the town to be preparing an LWRP, and construction requires an approved
or substantially complete LWRP, so a first application would almost
certainly be an LWRP/component planning grant. $75,000 minimum request.
Needs confirmation of waterway status and of any existing LWRP.

## Effort

- CFA submission; LWRP Budget Summary & Detail spreadsheet attachment;
  MWBE Compliance Form; Gender-Based Violence certification.
- Construction projects need draft/final designs, permit progress, and
  proof of public ownership or permanent public interest (deed/tax
  roll).
- Volunteer services and grant administration each capped at lesser of
  15% of award or $50,000.
- Projects must be completable within 5 years of contract.

## Notes
