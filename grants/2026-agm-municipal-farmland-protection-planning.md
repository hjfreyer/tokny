## Summary

Small planning grant to develop a municipal agricultural and farmland
protection plan, focused on protecting farmland through zoning, land
use and subdivision rules that are not unreasonably restrictive to farm
operations. Having an approved plan is also the gate to the much larger
Farmland Protection Implementation Grants (see
2026-agm-farmland-protection-implementation.md). RFA 0263 page says
"Applications are taken on a rolling basis" with no deadline listed.

## Eligibility

Program page (https://agriculture.ny.gov/land-and-water/farmland-protection-planning-grants-program,
read 2026-11-04): municipalities ("cities, towns, and villages") may
apply "only if they have not previously prepared an agricultural and
farmland protection plan"; two municipalities may apply jointly. Local
share must be at least 20% cash, no more than 80% in-kind. Municipal
award "up to $25,000 or 75% of cost, whichever is less."

**Verdict: Town of Kingston is eligible if it has never prepared an
agricultural and farmland protection plan** — needs confirmation.

## Fit

Cheap (≈$8,000 local share on a $33,000 plan) and a prerequisite for
easement funding. Whether the town has working farms, an existing plan,
or an interest in farmland protection needs confirmation; Ulster County
has a county plan and an Agricultural and Farmland Protection Board that
would be the natural partner.
