## Summary

FEMA's equipment-and-training grant for fire departments and
non-affiliated EMS: personal protective equipment, emergency response
vehicles, training to recognized standards, facility retrofits for
health hazards, and health/wellness programs. The FY2025 round
(DHS-25-GPD-083-00-99 family) opened May 19, 2026 and closed June 22,
2026, with $291.6M for AFG. The Federal Register notice scheduled for
September 10, 2026
(https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2026-18419.pdf) gives
the cost-share tiers: at least 5% non-federal for populations of
20,000 or fewer, 10% up to 1 million, 15% above; in-kind match is not
allowed for AFG (trade-in value of owned assets may be accepted
case-by-case). Recipients must also maintain aggregate spending on
allowable activities at ≥80% of the prior two-year average. Applied
through FEMA GO (go.fema.gov). Annual program; FY2026 round expected
spring 2027.

## Eligibility

grants.gov listing (read 2026-11-04): "City or township governments,"
county governments, tribal governments, 501(c)(3) nonprofits, and
"Other," with the additional note "Fire Departments, State Fire
Training Academies, Nonaffiliated Emergency Medical Services." The
FEMA advisory of May 18, 2026 describes AFG as for "fire departments
and other EMS agencies."

**Verdict:** A town government is an eligible applicant type, but the
substantive applicant is a fire department. Whether the Town of
Kingston operates a fire department itself, or whether fire protection
is provided by an independent fire district / fire company that would
apply on its own, needs confirmation. If the latter, the town is not
the applicant and this file should be passed to the district.

## Fit

Strong in kind for any small volunteer department (turnout gear, SCBA,
a pumper). Fit for the *town* depends entirely on the governance
question above — needs confirmation.

## Effort

FEMA GO application during a ~5-week window; FEMA's advisory says a
live webinar is offered during the window and encourages reviewing its
published resources first. Needs SAM.gov/UEI, a 5% cash match, and
the maintenance-of-effort commitment described above.
