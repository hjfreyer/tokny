## Summary

NYPA's statewide program to convert municipal streetlights to LED. NYPA
provides "a single point of contact," full turnkey services (lighting
audit, engineering and design, bidding and procurement, construction
management, environmental services), "low-rate financing for qualified
customers, including financing to purchase the streetlight system," and
the option to bundle the streetlight project with other energy
efficiency projects. A **Smart City Technology Grant Program** (the
"newest addition, with applications open") funds smart-city hardware,
software and labor tied to the streetlight system; an **Outdoor Lighting
O&M Service** is "Coming Soon." A 2021 state announcement
(https://lansingstar.com/news-archive/15792-7-5-million-available-for-municipalities-for-smart-street-lighting)
described $5 million set aside for municipalities other than the five
largest cities, requests up to $500,000, for municipalities that partner
with NYPA on LED conversion; the current NYPA page gives no dollar
figures.

This is not a cash grant: NYPA fronts the cost and the municipality
repays it from energy and maintenance savings. It is filed because it
is the standard mechanism by which small NY towns have converted their
lights, and it stacks with utility and NYSERDA incentives.

## Eligibility

The NYPA page is addressed to municipalities ("Municipalities across New
York State" in the 2021 announcement); financing is for "qualified
customers," a term the page does not define. To buy streetlights from
the utility that owns them, "follow the transfer process under Section
70-a" of the Public Service Law (PSC guidelines, order of 2016-10-13);
the PSC has approved such sales for Central Hudson customers (Village
of New Paltz, 2020:
https://dps.ny.gov/psc-approves-streetlighting-sale-9-upstate-municipalities).

**Verdict:** A town is the intended customer. Eligible.

## Fit

Only relevant if the Town pays for streetlights (either utility-owned
lights on a Central Hudson lighting tariff, or town-owned lights). How
many streetlights the Town has, who owns them, and the annual lighting
bill all need confirmation; a town of ~900 may have very few.

## Effort

- Submit the Smart Street Lighting NY Interest Form (on the NYPA page)
  or email SmartStreetLightingNY@NYPA.gov.
- For the grant: Smart City Grant Application Form (on the page).
- If Central Hudson owns the lights: Section 70-a purchase process
  through the PSC, which requires town board action and a utility
  valuation; NYPA can finance the purchase.
- A NYPA customer agreement/financing agreement will require a board
  resolution.
