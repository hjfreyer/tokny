## Summary

Rolling CDBG round (~$20M) that "does not share the annual CFA
deadline": opened March 20, 2026, submissions accepted through
December 31, 2026, awards rolling until funds run out. Categories:
**Public Facilities** (acquisition, construction, rehabilitation of
public facilities incl. ADA barrier removal), **Community Planning**
(needs assessments and project-readiness plans such as preliminary
design and cost estimates, prepared by a third-party professional),
Stand-Alone Business Assistance, Microenterprise, and **Imminent
Threat** (water/sewer repairs, storm drainage, flood walls, roads,
sidewalks, culverts, demolition after an event). Public infrastructure
and housing are separate RFAs. The open round has recurred annually.

## Eligibility

2026 Open Round RFA (read 2026-11-04): "Non-entitlement units of
general local government, including counties, towns, cities, and
villages" (under 50,000 population). Every activity must meet a HUD
national objective; for Community Planning "at least 51% of
beneficiaries be LMI, or that the plan addresses a slum or blighted
area." Imminent Threat may use urgent need (conditions within 18 months
of the event) or slum/blight (formal municipal designation). Applicants
must be current on state filings and not debarred.

**Verdict: Town of Kingston is eligible** as a non-entitlement town; the
LMI (51%) test on the service area is the gate for most activities.

## Fit

Community Planning ($50k, no match) for a preliminary engineering/cost
study of a town facility or drainage problem is the most accessible
piece; Public Facilities could fund an ADA retrofit of town hall or a
senior/community facility if the service area qualifies as LMI. Whether
the town's census geography meets 51% LMI needs confirmation (HUD LMISD
data); if not, an income survey is required.

## Effort

- Two-step: pre-application in the CFA portal (pre-submission
  certification, budget, map) → OCR Accept/Reject → full application
  within 60 days.
- Public hearing before a quorum of the full board before applying.
- Public Facilities need a preliminary conditions analysis and third-
  party cost estimate; draft NEPA/SEQRA record.
- Scoring: Need 30%, Impact 20%, Administrative Capacity 30% (60%
  minimum), Commitment/Readiness 20%; 65% overall minimum.
- Must execute grant within 45 days, request funds within 60 days, bid
  within 6 months; 25-month contract (13 months for planning).
