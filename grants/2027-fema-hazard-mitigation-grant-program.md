## Summary

Post-disaster mitigation money. After a presidential disaster
declaration anywhere in New York, FEMA makes HMGP funds available
statewide and DHSES runs a subapplication round. Eligible work per the
FEMA page: planning and enforcement (drafting/adopting mitigation
plans, property buyouts, post-disaster code enforcement); flood
protection (barriers, elevating structures, drainage improvements);
retrofits (strengthening buildings, upgrading utilities); and
construction (safe rooms, slope stabilization). "This grant funding is
available after a presidentially declared disaster."

## Eligibility

FEMA page (read 2026-11-04): homeowners and businesses cannot apply
directly — "a local community may apply for funding on their behalf."
Subapplicants work through the "state, tribe or territory (applicant
level) Hazard Mitigation Office." State, local, tribal and territorial
governments "must develop and adopt hazard mitigation plans to receive
funding for hazard mitigation project application." DHSES's hazard
mitigation page (https://www.dhses.ny.gov/hazard-mitigation) confirms a
FEMA-approved plan, updated every five years, is required; a
multi-jurisdictional plan becomes active for a participant once that
participant adopts it.

**Verdict:** The Town of Kingston is an eligible subapplicant via
DHSES whenever a NY disaster declaration opens an HMGP round, if it has
adopted the current Ulster County multi-jurisdictional hazard
mitigation plan (needs confirmation).

## Fit

Same project types as BRIC (culverts, drainage, road flood protection,
town-hall/garage retrofits) but less competitive because the pot is
state-level, not national. Whether the town has a shovel-ready
mitigation project needs confirmation. DHSES's page says most
construction projects must pass a FEMA Benefit-Cost Analysis.

## Effort

Watch DHSES announcements after any NY declaration (past rounds, e.g.
DR-4615 and DR-4694, are listed on the DHSES site). Subapplication via
FEMA GO with a BCA, scope, cost estimate, environmental/historic review
information and the mitigation plan adoption. DHSES expects to announce
programs in August/September per its page. Contact:
HazardMitigation@dhses.ny.gov, (518) 292-2304 (from DHSES's FY2024 FMA
announcement).
