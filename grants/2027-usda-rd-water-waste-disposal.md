## Summary

Funds clean and reliable drinking water systems, sanitary sewage
disposal, solid waste disposal, and storm water drainage in rural areas.
Eligible uses are acquiring, building, or improving drinking water
sourcing, treatment, storage and distribution; sewer collection,
transmission, treatment and disposal; solid waste collection, disposal
and closure; and storm water collection, transmission and disposal.
Related costs (legal, engineering, land and water rights, start-up O&M,
construction-period interest) may be covered. Loans run up to 40 years
at fixed rates (Oct–Dec 2026 rates: Poverty 2.875%, Intermediate
3.750%, Market 4.750%). Grants are available only when funds allow and
are combined with a loan to keep user costs reasonable.

## Eligibility

From the program page (read 2026-11-04): "Most state and local
governmental entities, private nonprofits, and federally-recognized
tribes" may apply; the program "assists qualified applicants who are
not otherwise able to obtain commercial credit on reasonable terms."
Eligible areas: "rural areas and towns with a population of 10,000 or
less," tribal lands, and colonias.

**Verdict:** The Town of Kingston (~900 residents) is a local
governmental entity in an eligible area and qualifies. Whether it has
or wants a water, sewer, storm water, or solid waste facility to fund
needs confirmation.

## Fit

Only relevant if the town owns or plans a water/sewer district, a
storm water drainage project, or a solid waste facility — needs
confirmation. Storm water collection and disposal is the use most
likely to touch a highway department.

## Effort

Program page says to speak with a state program specialist before
completing forms (state-specific requirements apply), then file
electronically through RD Apply or the local RD office. Requires SAM.gov
registration and a demonstration that commercial credit is
unavailable. (Town-side, not from the notice: a loan component would
need a board resolution and bonding authority.)
Pair with a SEARCH grant (see
`2027-usda-rd-search-grant.md`) for the feasibility/engineering work
that precedes an application.
