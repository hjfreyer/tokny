## Summary

Not a grant. HCR certification that a locality supports housing growth,
required to apply to a growing list of discretionary programs. Two
pathways: (1) growth metrics — permits showing housing stock growth of
0.33% (upstate) over the past year or 1% (upstate) over three years; or
(2) commitment pledge — submit the same data and have the town board
pass HCR's Pro-Housing Resolution "as written." Rolling; HCR decides
within 90 days of submission. Certified localities resubmit permit data
and zoning updates annually.

Programs the page says require certification: Downtown Revitalization
Initiative, NY Forward, **Regional Council Capital Fund**, **New York
Main Street**, Long Island Investment Fund, **Mid-Hudson Momentum Fund**,
Public Transportation Modernization Enhancement Program, and **Clean
Water for Housing** (see 2026-efc-clean-water-for-housing.md). The
sources/ny-cfa.md note that it gates the largest CFA pot is consistent
with this.

## Eligibility

Program page (read 2026-11-04): open to local governments; no size
restriction is stated. The zoning-summary template differs for
municipalities with seven or fewer zoning districts (a simpler option
for small towns).

**Verdict: Town of Kingston can apply** via the resolution pathway
regardless of growth history. Whether it is already certified needs
confirmation (HCR publishes a certified list).

## Fit

Low cost, board-level decision, and increasingly a prerequisite; the
trade-off is the political content of the required resolution. The town
has a town board and clerk who could assemble the submission. Whether
the board is willing needs confirmation.

## Effort

- Letter of intent from an authorized official to the program email.
- Zoning map in GIS-readable format (shapefile) and a zoning code
  summary (short form if ≤7 districts).
- Building permit data for the prior five years.
- Executed Pro-Housing Resolution (Option 2).
- Upload via Procorem; annual resubmission thereafter.
