## Summary

Earmarks. Each spring, members of Congress accept project requests
from local governments and nonprofits for inclusion in the next fiscal
year's appropriations bills; a small number are funded. For FY2027,
Sen. Gillibrand's office accepted preliminary requests through its
online CDS Request Form with a deadline of "11:59 PM EST on Sunday,
March 22" (2026), and said "only a handful of projects may actually be
funded." Sen. Schumer's page
(https://www.schumer.senate.gov/congressionally-directed-spending)
lists nine FY2027 bills open to requests (Agriculture-Rural
Development; Commerce-Justice-Science; Energy & Water; Financial
Services; Homeland Security; Interior-Environment; Labor-HHS;
MilCon-VA; Transportation-HUD) and is "no longer accepting requests"
for FY2027. Rep. Pat Ryan (NY-18) ran a House Community Project
Funding process with a March 17 deadline for FY2024
(https://patryan.house.gov/media/press-releases/congressman-pat-ryan-announces-application-period-community-project-funding),
asking for "evidence of community support and need" such as
stakeholder letters or inclusion in a local planning document. The
FY2028 cycle should open in roughly February–March 2027.

## Eligibility

Gillibrand FY27 page (read 2026-11-04): for "state, local, and tribal
governments and nonprofit organizations" with projects in New York;
"only state and local governments and eligible non-profit entities are
permitted to receive funding." Schumer: eligible recipients are "state
and local government entities and nonprofit organizations"; for-profits
are not eligible. Ryan (FY24): "nonprofit organizations and state,
local, tribal, and territorial governments."

**Verdict:** The Town of Kingston is eligible to submit to both
senators and its House member. Whether the town lies in NY-18 (Rep.
Ryan) needs confirmation against the current district map.

## Fit

Best suited to a single, well-defined capital need with a clear
appropriations bill behind it — e.g. a town hall/highway garage
project (Agriculture-Rural Development bill), a water/sewer project
(Interior-Environment bill), or a road/bridge (Transportation-HUD
bill). Requires community-support
evidence, so a project already in a town plan scores better. Whether
the town has such a project needs confirmation.

## Effort

Online forms with each office (Gillibrand: CDS Request Form;
questions to appropriations@gillibrand.senate.gov or a district
office; Ryan: forms at patryan.house.gov/services/community-project-funding-requests,
questions (202) 225-5614). Gillibrand's page says requestors "may be
asked for additional information later" and that the Senate
Appropriations Committee's final guidance may change the rules. Ryan's
process asks for letters of support or planning-document references.
Funded projects are subject to GAO audit (Schumer). Plan to have the
project description, cost estimate and board support ready by
February.
