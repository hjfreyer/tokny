## Summary

NYSDOT's competitive program for rehabilitation and replacement of
municipally owned bridges and culverts, funding "all phases of project
delivery, including design, right-of-way acquisition and construction"
(governor's release, July 11, 2024: $484M to 216 projects in 137
communities). Bridges must be on a public roadway carrying vehicles and
federal-aid eligible (federal-aid process); culverts must be on a public
highway and follow the state-aid process. Scoring weighs resiliency,
significance (traffic volume, detour length) and structural condition.
Solicited in multi-year rounds (2016, 2018, 2020/21, 2022/23, 2024); the
SFY 2026-27 enacted budget includes $200M for BRIDGE NY (NY Association
of Towns CHIPS guide), so a new solicitation is anticipated but no date
was found.

## Eligibility

NYSDOT's BRIDGE NY page could not be fetched on 2026-11-04 (blocked);
the following is from secondary sources. LaBella summary
(https://labellapc.com/insights/the-next-round-of-bridge-ny/): the
program is "open to all municipal owners of bridges and culverts."
Governor's 2024 release: awards went to "137 local governments" including
counties, towns, villages and a city; the Town of Brookhaven received
$910,000 for a culvert. Ulster County DPW received $5M for the Denning
Road bridge in 2024.

**Verdict: Town of Kingston is eligible as a municipal owner of
culverts/bridges on town highways** — confirm against the official
solicitation when posted.

## Fit

The single best match for a highway department with undersized or
deteriorated culverts; past rounds funded many town culverts. Whether
the town owns culverts that would score (poor condition, long detour)
needs confirmation; inventory via the highway superintendent.

## Effort

- Applications are submitted during a solicitation, with selections
  informed by Regional/Metropolitan Planning Organizations (UCTC for
  Ulster County).
- Culvert projects use the state-aid process; bridges use the federal-
  aid process (NEPA, Local Projects Manual).
- Expect a board resolution and condition documentation.
