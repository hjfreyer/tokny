## Summary

Small grants that help smaller institutions holding humanities
collections — the 2025 NOFO
(https://www.humanities.gov/sites/default/files/2024-10/PAG%202025.pdf)
names assessments and planning (preservation assessments, written
plans/policies), preventive care (storage supplies, digital storage,
reversible environmental improvements, environmental monitoring, pest
management, staff training) and collections management (surveys,
inventories, condition reports). Not funded: building preservation,
capital improvements, digitization, conservation treatment,
appraisals. Consultants are encouraged. Aimed especially at
institutions that have never received an NEH grant. Annual program
with a January deadline: 2025 NOFO deadline was January 9, 2025; the
grants.gov forecast (Assistance Listing 45.149) estimated a January 8,
2026 deadline for the next cycle.

## Eligibility

grants.gov listing (read 2026-11-04): "City or township governments,"
county, state, special district and tribal governments, 501(c)(3)
nonprofits, and institutions of higher education. The 2025 NOFO lists
"a state or local government or one of their agencies" as eligible.
NEH's 2023 guidelines blog
(https://www.neh.gov/blog/whats-new-preservation-assistance-grants-updated-guidelines-out-now)
explicitly names "town and county records offices" among the intended
applicants. Cost share is not required.

**Verdict:** The Town of Kingston (via its clerk's office or town
historian) is eligible. It needs a humanities collection to care for —
historic town records, maps, photographs, a historian's archive. What
the town holds needs confirmation.

## Fit

A $10k grant for a preservation assessment of the town's historic
records, archival boxes/folders, and a data logger is almost exactly
what the program exists for; the only question is whether the town has
materials worth the effort — needs confirmation. A town historical
society (if one exists) could apply instead as a nonprofit.

## Effort

grants.gov application: narrative (the NOFO lets projects not based
on a prior assessment omit that section), budget, SAM.gov/UEI. No
match.
