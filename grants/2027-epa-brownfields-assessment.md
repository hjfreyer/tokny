## Summary

Funds inventories, Phase I/II environmental site assessments, cleanup
planning and community outreach for brownfield sites — abandoned or
underused properties where real or perceived contamination complicates
reuse (old garages, mills, gas stations, dumps, farm structures). EPA's
FY2027 competition for Assessment, Revolving Loan Fund and Cleanup
grants is open with an application deadline of January 21, 2027
(page last updated November 2, 2026). Multipurpose grants are not
being offered in FY2027. Grants.gov guideline IDs: Community-wide
Assessment 364198, Assessment Coalition 364199. EPA is holding
narrative-criteria webinars on November 9, 2026 (assessment grants).
The EPA types-of-funding page
(https://www.epa.gov/brownfields/types-funding) states community-wide
assessment grants are up to $500,000, coalitions up to $1,000,000.

## Eligibility

EPA's "Entities Eligible to Receive Brownfield Grants" list (read
2026-11-04 via the archived copy at
https://19january2025snapshot.epa.gov/brownfields/entities-eligible-receive-brownfield-grants;
the live page returned an error) includes a "General Purpose Unit of
Local Government," defined as "a county, municipality, city, town,
township, local public authority..." Assessment grants carry no cost
share. The UConn TAB summary of EPA's FY27 notice
(https://tab.program.uconn.edu/2026/05/21/fy27-epa-brownfield-grants-notice/)
says applications are due ~60 days after solicitation and that EPA
expected ~30 community-wide assessment awards.

**Verdict:** The Town of Kingston is a general purpose unit of local
government and is eligible. The town does not need to own the sites
for an assessment grant.

## Fit

Only worth pursuing if the town has at least a short list of
suspected brownfield properties it wants reused (needs confirmation).
A coalition application led by Ulster County or a neighboring town
would spread the administrative load; whether the county is applying
needs confirmation.

## Effort

Full federal application via grants.gov by 2027-01-21 against the
guidelines at grants.gov 364198 (not readable from this workspace):
expect a narrative against EPA's ranking criteria, threshold documents,
and SAM.gov/UEI. EPA's narrative-criteria webinar for assessment
grants is November 9, 2026. The EPA page notes that current
recipients must have drawn down 70% of prior assessment funds by
October 1, 2026 to reapply — not relevant to a first-time applicant.
