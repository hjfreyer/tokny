## Summary

EPF-funded planning grants for a new or updated **town comprehensive
plan** (Town Law §272-a), community-wide zoning implementing an existing
plan, zoning amendments for part of a municipality, or an area/hamlet
plan (with or without follow-on zoning). One plan per application. All
projects must be consistent with DOS's ten Smart Growth Principles and
incorporate child-care-friendly planning and zoning practices. 2026-27
round (RFA #26-OPDSG-18) was due 4:00 pm, July 31, 2026 via the CFA;
expected again in the 2027 CFA.

## Eligibility

RFA (read 2026-11-04): eligible applicants are "Towns, counties,
villages, and cities"; counties or regional planning entities acting on
behalf of a municipality; prequalified not-for-profits acting on behalf
of a municipality; and joint applications. State share capped at 75% of
eligible cost (85% if in an EJ Area/DAC, if >20% of households are
housing-cost burdened, or if the area includes a child care desert).
Match cannot be other EPF money (WQIP, BOA, LWRP, OPRHP EPF).

**Verdict: Town of Kingston is eligible** (towns named first).

## Fit

Strong candidate if the town's comprehensive plan is old or absent —
many small Ulster towns have used this for plan updates. The $75,000
minimum request implies a ~$100,000 project with ~$25,000 local match
(cash or documented staff time). Whether the town has a current
comprehensive plan or zoning needs confirmation.

## Effort

- CFA submission by the July deadline; regional workshops/webinars in
  June.
- Work schedule with milestones; multiple consultant quotes encouraged;
  match must be documented and secured in the application.
- MWBE Compliance Form; Gender-Based Violence certification; (EO 16
  only for non-municipal applicants).
- Reimbursement basis, no advance; contract term up to 3 years;
  procurement under GML §103/§104-b.
- Scoring (120 pts): need/distress 20, smart growth commitment 12,
  public engagement 14, principles + child care zoning 44, capacity 6,
  budget 24.
