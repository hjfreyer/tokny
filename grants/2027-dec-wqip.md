## Summary

WQIP is DEC's competitive, statewide reimbursement grant for projects
that improve water quality or protect drinking water. 2026 (Round 22)
categories: wastewater treatment improvement; non-agricultural nonpoint
source abatement and control (green infrastructure, stormwater
retrofits, streambank/shoreline stabilization, riparian buffers, stream
culvert repair and replacement); vacuum trucks for MS4s; land acquisition
for source water protection; **salt storage and road salt reduction**;
dam safety repair/removal; aquatic connectivity (culverts, bridges,
dams); marine habitat restoration; fish and wildlife habitat; landfill
leachate treatment.

Round 22 closed July 31, 2026; awards (over $246M to 139 projects) were
announced Oct 30, 2026. The program page says applications are
"expected to open for Round 23 in May 2027" through the CFA.

## Eligibility

Program page (read 2026-11-04): "Municipalities* (including Indian
Nations) are eligible for all project types." The footnote defines
municipality to include a county, city, town, village, school district
and others (ECL §56-0101 municipal corporations). Soil and Water
Conservation Districts are eligible for all types except wastewater, dam
safety and landfill leachate; 501(c)(3)s only for a subset.

May 2026 fact sheet
(https://dec.ny.gov/sites/default/files/2026-06/DECFactsheetWQIPMay2026.pdf):
"All grants require a local match of 25% of the requested grant amount";
match can be cash and/or in-kind, cannot come from other NYS funds;
federal funds may be used.

**Verdict: Town of Kingston is eligible** for all categories.

## Fit

Salt storage (a covered salt shed for the highway department), road salt
reduction, and culvert repair/replacement under non-ag nonpoint source or
aquatic connectivity are classic small-town WQIP projects. Whether the
town has an uncovered salt pile, failing culverts, or a 25% match needs
confirmation.

## Effort

- CFA application when Round 23 opens (expected May 2027).
- All applicants: WQIP budget worksheet, location map (1:24,000 USGS),
  sexual harassment prevention certification, EO 16 certification,
  gender-based violence certification.
- Category-specific: salt storage uses OGS specifications and aquifer
  maps; road salt reduction needs a public highway lane/water resource
  map and winter road maintenance outline; culvert projects need photos
  of erosion or a DEC-approved site assessment; aquatic connectivity
  needs a NAACC assessment; wastewater needs a PE-stamped engineering
  report.
- Reimbursement grant: town fronts costs.
