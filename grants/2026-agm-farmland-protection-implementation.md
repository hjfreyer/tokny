## Summary

Pays for the purchase of perpetual agricultural conservation easements
on viable farmland (easement value by appraisal, plus transaction costs
such as surveys, title, appraisals, legal, baseline documentation,
stewardship fees), with 10% incentive payments for Climate Resiliency or
Source Water Protection projects. Round 20 (RFA0393, SFS event FPIG20):
applications "accepted on a continuous basis until funds are committed
or the RFA closes"; awards began mid-March 2026. The farmland protection
page says "a record level of funding of $67 million is currently
available."

## Eligibility

RFA (read 2026-11-04): eligible applicants include a county
agricultural and farmland protection board (with an approved county
plan); "a municipality, including a town, with a local farmland
protection plan" (the county board must endorse by letter); or a
not-for-profit conservation organization or SWCD assisting a county or
municipality (county board and municipality both endorse). Project must
be consistent with the county or municipal plan; the applicant is the
easement grantee; at least one farm operation; easement perpetual.

**Verdict: Town of Kingston is eligible only if it has an adopted
local agricultural and farmland protection plan** (needs confirmation;
if not, see the $25k planning grant) — otherwise it can only partner
with Ulster County's board or a land trust as the applicant.

## Fit

Easement acquisition is a big-ticket, specialist process; a small town
would normally let a land trust or the county hold the easement. Worth
filing because a willing farm owner in the town could be served through
a county/land-trust application with a town endorsement letter. Needs
confirmation of farms and of any local plan.

## Effort

- Application through the SFS Vendor Portal (town must be SFS
  registered); read "How to Apply for FPIG20 in SFS" first.
- Appraisal, title work, survey, baseline documentation, endorsement
  letters from the county board (and municipality if a nonprofit
  applies).
- Match: 12.5% landowner-only with the Department's model easement, or
  25% local with ≥5%/$5,000 cash not from the landowner alone nor from
  state sources.
