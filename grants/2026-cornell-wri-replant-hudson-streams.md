## Summary

A Request for Proposals administered by the NYS Water Resources
Institute (Cornell) with the Hudson River Estuary Program, coordinated
with the Hudson Estuary Trees for Tribs program. The DEC grants page
lists the 2027 scope as: riparian plantings, applications for 2028
plantings, a municipal or watershed riparian assessment, and outreach
on riparian protection. The RFP itself is a Cornell Box link
(https://cornell.app.box.com/s/w31jarbmz2s4bz7mdc02aan3yuor1er4) that
could not be read on 2026-11-04.

## Eligibility

DEC page: "Applicants must be located in and work within the Hudson
River Estuary Program boundary." Applicant *types* are not stated on
the DEC page; the RFP must be read to confirm that a town government
may apply directly. The DEC page describes the related Small Grants for
Watershed Education and Stewardship (NEIWPCC, up to $10,000, due
2026-12-04) as "community-led projects" with applicant types also
unstated.

**Verdict:** Unverified. Geography is likely fine (Ulster County);
whether a municipality is an eligible applicant must be confirmed in
the RFP before any effort is spent.

## Fit

Streambank plantings on town-owned land (highway right-of-way along a
creek, a town park) or a town-wide riparian assessment. Whether the
Town owns any stream frontage needs confirmation.

## Effort

Unknown until the RFP is read. Deadline 5:00 p.m. 2026-11-25.
