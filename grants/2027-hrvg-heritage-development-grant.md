## Summary

Small grants from the Hudson River Valley National Heritage Area (NHA)
for "programs and exhibits that interpret, preserve, and celebrate the
NHA's cultural and natural resources." Projects must be "permanent or
semi-permanent," occur within NHA boundaries, exemplify an NHA theme
(Nature & Culture; Freedom & Dignity; Corridor of Commerce), deliver
tourism and economic benefit, and encourage local/regional partnerships.
Partner organizations receive priority consideration.

## Eligibility

Program page: the program supports "municipalities and nonprofit
organizations." The NHA page (https://hudsongreenway.ny.gov/national-heritage-area)
says the NHA "stretches from Yonkers to Troy, including 250 communities
in 10 counties boarding the Hudson River" but does not list the
communities. Whether the Town of Kingston (which is in Ulster County but
not itself on the river) is inside the NHA boundary needs confirmation
with the Greenway. The November 2025 guidelines document on the page
holds the full eligibility rules and was not read.

**Verdict:** Towns are named as eligible applicants; geographic
inclusion of the Town of Kingston in the NHA is unconfirmed.

## Fit

Small money for interpretive signage, a historical marker exhibit, or a
town-hall history display. Whether the Town has a historian, historic
sites, or an interpretive need is unknown and needs confirmation.

## Effort

- Application through hrvg.grantplatform.com.
- Grant Application Budget form; town board resolution approving and
  endorsing the application (same documents as the Trail grants); Match
  Worksheet.
- "Trail and NHA Grant Claim for Payment, Match Worksheet, and Samples"
  file governs reimbursement.
