## Summary

Environmental Bond Act (2022) grant for **construction** of recreational
facilities at municipally owned parks and sites: playgrounds, courts,
playing fields, swimming, biking, boating, picnicking, hiking, fishing or
camping facilities. Grant covers up to 90% of eligible cost;
reimbursement-based; one location per application; planning/design may
be in the budget but not alone. Second round: $20M program, "up to $10
million to be awarded in 2026"; page says "Application cycle closed."
No next-round date is given; the remaining Bond Act allocation suggests
a further cycle, so treat as anticipated.

## Eligibility

Program page (read 2026-11-04): "Counties, Cities, Towns, and Villages"
within New York State that own the property where the project is sited.

**Verdict: Town of Kingston is eligible** for a project on town-owned
park/recreation land.

## Fit

90/10 is the most generous park match in the state; a new playground,
pavilion, ballfield or trailhead at a town-owned park would fit. Whether
the town owns park land and has a construction-ready project needs
confirmation.

## Effort

- CFA application ("Municipal Parks and Recreation Grants" under
  Programs) when a cycle opens; read the MPR Program Guidance Document.
- Supporting documents referenced on the page: authorizing resolution,
  opinion of municipal counsel, survey map, cost summary template, SFS
  registration, state certifications.
- Contact the Regional Grants Administrator for Ulster County or
  518-474-0427.
