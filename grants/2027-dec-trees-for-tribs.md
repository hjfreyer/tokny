## Summary

Not a cash grant: DEC supplies free native trees and shrubs for
"qualifying planting projects along the tributary streams" inside the
Hudson River Estuary grant boundary, with staff help on plant selection
and planting plans. Rolling annual deadlines: **March 1 for spring
planting, August 1 for fall planting**; apply early for large sites or
specific species. Next window: March 1, 2027.

## Eligibility

Program page (read 2026-11-04): "if you own or manage property near a
stream" within the Hudson River Estuary grant boundary you can apply.
The page does not list applicant types; a town that owns or manages
streamside land (road right-of-way, park, town hall parcel) would be an
owner/manager. Geographic eligibility is by the boundary map linked from
the page; Ulster County is not named on the page.

**Verdict: likely eligible** if the town owns/manages a streamside
site inside the estuary boundary — confirm the site on DEC's boundary
map.

## Fit

Zero-cost riparian buffer planting along a town-owned stream bank or
eroding road shoulder; pairs with a WQIP or Tributary Restoration
project. Whether the town has such a site and volunteers to plant it
needs confirmation.

## Effort

- Complete the Hudson Estuary Trees for Tribs application (PDF on the
  page); read the eligibility guidelines and maintenance guide.
- Applicant is "responsible for preparing, planting, and maintaining the
  site" and is encouraged to recruit volunteers.
