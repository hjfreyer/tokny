## Summary

The state's main construction grant for municipally owned wastewater
and drinking water infrastructure. 2026 round: $425M announced June 10,
2026; applications due July 27, 2026; ~$500M in awards to 106 projects
announced Oct 20, 2026. The page gives no date for the next round
("contact the Community Assistance Teams or sign up for EFC emails"),
but the program has run every year since 2015 with a June opening and
late-July deadline.

Key 2026 terms (Grant Summary,
https://efc.ny.gov/state-water-grants-summary-pdf): clean water WIIA =
lesser of $25M or 25% of net eligible costs; **enhanced 50% for a
"small, rural, and disadvantaged community of 3,500 or fewer people"
meeting the 2026 Clean Water Hardship Policy**; drinking water WIIA =
70% up to $5M for most projects, 100% up to $2M for lead service line
inventories, 70% up to $10M for LSL replacement, 70% uncapped for PFAS
above MCL; max two projects per municipality per round; IMG = 40% up to
$30M, needs an intermunicipal agreement with a lead municipality.

## Eligibility

ENB notice 2026-06-10: "A WIIA grant or IMG grant is available only to a
municipality, which is defined to include a county, city, town,
village," public benefit corporations/authorities, school districts,
Indian Nations and state agencies empowered to build water quality
infrastructure. Grant Summary: projects must be at "municipally-owned
sewage treatment works or municipally-owned public water systems."
Municipalities "do not need SRF financing or a listing on the CWSRF or
DWSRF Intended Use Plan to apply."

**Verdict: Town of Kingston is eligible** (towns named), but only for
infrastructure the town (or a town district) owns.

## Fit

Depends entirely on whether the town owns any water or sewer system —
needs confirmation. If it does, the ≤3,500-population hardship tier
(50% grant) is designed for towns this size. IMG could apply to a shared
project with a neighboring town or the county.

## Effort

- Online application at www.efc.ny.gov/WIIA during the round; combined
  application available for projects also seeking SRF financing.
- Required: Engineering Report conforming to EFC's outline with schedule
  and budget; environmental review documentation; SHPO Project Review
  Determination Letter; bond resolution or authorizing resolution;
  intermunicipal agreement for joint projects.
- Projects must not have issued a Notice to Proceed before Oct 1, 2024
  or completed construction before June 10, 2026 (2026 rule; expect
  similar dates in 2027).
- Scoring: public health/water quality, MHI, population served,
  community support, DAC status, readiness.
