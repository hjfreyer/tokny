## Summary

One of four 2026 Hudson River Estuary Program RFAs (total $1.25M, EPF).
Local Stewardship Planning funds "climate adaptation and resilience
planning, habitat conservation planning, and watershed-based stewardship
planning for estuary tributaries" (DEC press release, Sept 2026). Projects
must implement priorities of the 2026–2030 Hudson River Estuary Action
Agenda. Competitive; applications through the SFS Grants Management
system, application ID HRER51. Deadline 3:00 pm, December 17, 2026.

## Eligibility

ENB notice 2026-10-07
(https://dec.ny.gov/news/environmental-notice-bulletin/2026-10-07/public-notice/statewide-2026-hudson-river-estuary-grants-for-local-stewardship-planning-under-the-new-york-state-environmental-protection-fund):
eligible applicants are "Governmental Entities and Not-For-Profit
Corporations located within the Geographic Boundaries of the Hudson River
Estuary." The program grants page lists "Municipalities and 501(c)(3)
nonprofits within program boundaries" and, in its general eligibility
section, governmental entities, soil and water conservation districts,
school districts, community colleges and 501(c)(3)s. Match: "at least
15% of the grant amount."

Geographic eligibility is by the Estuary Watershed Boundary (interactive
map on the grants page). The companion Tributary Restoration RFA
(dec.ny.gov/media/72406) describes the estuary as Troy dam to the
Verrazano Narrows "along with tributaries flowing into these waters."

**Verdict: Town of Kingston is eligible as a governmental entity,
provided the project location is inside the estuary watershed boundary**
— Ulster County drains to the Hudson, but confirm the town's location on
DEC's boundary map before applying.

## Fit

Small awards ($10.5k floor), 15% match, planning-only scope: a
flood/climate resilience plan, a natural resources inventory, or a
stream-corridor stewardship plan would fit. Whether the town has any such
plan already, or staff/consultant capacity, needs confirmation.

## Effort

- Register in NYS SFS Grants Management (grantsmanagement.ny.gov);
  nonprofits must prequalify, governments only register.
- Complete the Word bid form (HRE49–HRE52 series), convert to PDF, upload
  to SFS by 3:00 pm Dec 17, 2026.
- Information webinars were held Oct 8, 2026; recordings posted on DEC's
  site.
- The Local Stewardship Planning RFA PDF (dec.ny.gov/media/72401) could
  not be fetched on 2026-11-04; read it for required attachments
  (expect certifications similar to the Tributary RFA: sexual harassment
  prevention, EO 16, gender-based violence, Smart Growth form).
