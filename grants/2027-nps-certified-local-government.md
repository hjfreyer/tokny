## Summary

The CLG program, created by a 1980 amendment to the National Historic
Preservation Act and administered in each state by the SHPO, certifies
municipalities that run a local preservation program and then invites
them to apply each year for grants "for historic preservation work"
supporting "local municipal preservation initiatives" (the page does
not itemize eligible project types). NY SHPO's page says
applications "are usually due to the SHPO in the spring" with "Awards
... announced in the fall," and that amounts, parameters and deadlines
may change. Coordinator: Frances Stern, (518) 268-2219.

## Eligibility

NY SHPO CLG page (read 2026-11-04): "Any city, town, village, or
county" can apply for certification once SHPO determines it meets
state and federal standards. The community must "Have a regulatory
historic preservation ordinance in place" and "Appoint a qualified
review commission"; SHPO then forwards to NPS for formal
certification. The grants page
(https://www.parks.ny.gov/grants/certified-local-government/default.aspx)
says "active CLGs are invited to apply."

**Verdict:** The Town of Kingston is eligible to *become* a CLG, but
only certified CLGs can receive the grants. Whether the town has a
historic preservation law and commission, or is already a CLG, needs
confirmation; if not, this is a two-step path (adopt a local law and
commission, get certified, then apply).

## Fit

Worth it if the town has historic resources (e.g. a hamlet center,
churches, stone houses) it wants surveyed or listed — needs
confirmation. Certification also brings SHPO technical assistance.
Small dollars but minimal competition within the CLG pool.

## Effort

Certification: draft and adopt a preservation ordinance (SHPO provides
a Model Law PDF), appoint a commission, submit the certification
application (SHPO provides a Certification Process PDF). Grants: annual
spring application to SHPO; details not on the page — contact the
coordinator. The page links to the NPS CLG site but does not itself
name the federal funding source.
