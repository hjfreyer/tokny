## Summary

Small grants that pay for an engineering report (or inflow/infiltration
study) so a municipality can later qualify for Clean Water State
Revolving Fund financing or WIIA grants. The 2026 round closed June 12,
2026; "$3 million awarded to 63 projects statewide in September 2026"
(mostly $50,000 each per the governor's Sept 21, 2026 release). The page
lists no future round; EFC says to watch Community Assistance Teams or
its email list. The program has run annually (2025 RFA was noticed in
Feb 2025; 2026 in March/April 2026).

## Eligibility

Program page (read 2026-11-04): refers to "municipalities" and
"communities". Eligibility is by median household income: for
Mid-Hudson, Long Island and NYC, MHI "equal to or less than $110,000"
(ACS data). The governor's release describes recipients as local
governments including "villages, towns, cities, counties, and water or
sewer districts."

**Verdict: Town of Kingston is eligible if its ACS median household
income is ≤ $110,000** (Mid-Hudson threshold) — needs confirmation from
ACS data linked on the page.

## Fit

Only useful if the town owns or is contemplating a sewer system or
wastewater facility (or a sewer district). Whether the town has any
municipal wastewater infrastructure needs confirmation; if not, skip.

## Effort

- Grant Summary, Documentation Guidance, Engineering Report Outline and
  I&I Study Outline PDFs are on the page.
- Prior rounds were submitted through the CFA (the 2026 ENB notice of
  2026-03-18 announced the RFA; submission method not confirmed from the
  page).
- Expect a board resolution and SFS/EFC portal registration.

## Notes

- 2026-11-04: EFC Community Assistance Teams (https://efc.ny.gov/cat)
  offer free funding consultations to communities without current
  state-funded water projects — a good first call before any EFC
  application. Phone 518-402-7472.
