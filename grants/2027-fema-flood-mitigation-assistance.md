## Summary

FEMA's annual flood-specific mitigation grant, funded by the National
Flood Insurance Program. Per DHSES's FY2024 announcement (FEMA
announced April 22, 2026): three activity types — capability and
capacity building (mitigation plans, project scoping, and similar);
localized flood risk reduction projects (up to $50M federal per
project); and individual flood mitigation projects for NFIP-insured
structures (elevation, acquisition). The FY2024 round ran April 30 –
June 18, 2026 for NY subapplications (DHSES to FEMA August 6, 2026).
It is an annual program, so the next round is expected spring 2027.

## Eligibility

DHSES announcement (read 2026-11-04): eligible subapplicants are
"state agencies, local governments (cities, townships, counties, and
special districts), and federally recognized Indian/Native American
tribal governments." Individuals, businesses and nonprofits apply
through a subapplicant. Subapplicants "must participate in the NFIP and
not be withdrawn, on probation, or suspended," and need a FEMA-approved
local hazard mitigation plan by the application deadline (plan
development subapplications are exempt).

**Verdict:** The Town of Kingston is an eligible subapplicant type.
Two prerequisites need confirmation: NFIP participation in good
standing (check FEMA's Community Status Book) and adoption of the
current Ulster County hazard mitigation plan.

## Fit

Useful only if the town has flood-prone town-owned infrastructure or
NFIP-insured repetitive-loss properties it would sponsor for
elevation/buyout; needs confirmation. The project-scoping category
could fund the engineering for a drainage or culvert project before a
BRIC/HMGP construction application.

## Effort

Subapplication through FEMA GO (go.fema.gov) to DHSES. Needs UEI, EIN,
active SAM.gov registration, login.gov account and an Authorized
Organizational Representative in FEMA GO — DHSES says registration can
take up to four weeks. No pre-application or LOI. Management costs
capped at 5% for subapplicants. Contact HazardMitigation@dhses.ny.gov,
(518) 292-2304.
