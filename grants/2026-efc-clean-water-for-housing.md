## Summary

New (Oct 2026) EFC grant for drinking water, sewer or stormwater
projects that address capacity constraints impeding housing development
or rehabilitation. $175M statewide plus $50M "reserved for rural
communities" where aging or inadequate water systems are obstacles to
development. Rolling, first-come first-served until funds are gone; a
municipality may receive at most two awards. Construction should start
within one year of award and the related housing work within two.

## Eligibility

Program page (read 2026-11-04): a municipality that "is certified as a
Pro-Housing Community by NYS Homes and Community Renewal (HCR)", or that
has submitted a complete Pro-Housing application and is certified before
award; or a county with documentation that the project promotes housing
or lies in a certified Pro-Housing Community. Private and nonprofit
housing developers are ineligible. Governor's release (Oct 23, 2026):
"Municipalities certified through the State's Pro-Housing Communities
Program, and eligible counties."

**Verdict: eligible only if the Town of Kingston is (or becomes) a
certified Pro-Housing Community** — certification status needs
confirmation (see 2026-hcr-pro-housing-communities-certification.md).

## Fit

Requires both a municipal water/sewer system with a capacity constraint
and a housing project it blocks. Whether the town has either needs
confirmation; for a rural town without municipal water this is a pass.

## Effort

- Engineering report, project description and budget, environmental
  review documentation, and an explanation of the housing constraint,
  submitted via EFC's online portal.
- Pro-Housing certification (HCR, rolling, decided within 90 days of a
  complete submission) must be approved before award.
- EFC Community Assistance Teams offer one-on-one help.
