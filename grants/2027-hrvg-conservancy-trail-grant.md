## Summary

Grants for "creating or rehabilitating locally owned land and water
trail routes" within the Greenway Area, in three tiers: feasibility
study, engineering and design, and capital construction. Priority goes to
"projects that connect existing trail sections" and to trails designated
as part of the Greenway Trail System. Projects connecting downtowns,
historic sites, open spaces, and Hudson River access are named as the
target.

## Eligibility

Program page: "Municipalities and nonprofits" are eligible applicants.
Projects must be within the Greenway Area; Ulster County is one of the 14
Greenway counties (https://hudsongreenway.ny.gov/greenway-area-map).
The page does not say the applicant must be a designated Greenway
Community for this program (that requirement is stated only for the
Community Planning grants). The full Conservancy Trail Grant Guidelines
(linked from the page) were not read; confirm there.

**Verdict:** Town governments are named as eligible. Qualifies, subject
to the trail being locally owned and in the Greenway Area.

## Fit

Useful if the Town owns or wants to build a trail (rail trail link, park
loop, creek-side path). Whether the Town has any trail asset or plan
needs confirmation.

## Effort

- Grant Application Budget form (required for all applicants).
- Town board resolution "approving and endorsing the application"
  (sample provided; required for all applications).
- Match Worksheet (match terms are in the guidelines, not on the page).
- Submit through hrvg.grantplatform.com.
- Page suggests: plan the project, identify connecting trails, identify
  partners, then submit.
