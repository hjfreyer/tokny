## Summary

Funds the actual remediation of a brownfield site that the applicant
owns. Part of EPA's FY2027 competition (deadline January 21, 2027;
grants.gov guideline ID 364202). Cleanup grants are generally limited
to $500,000 under CERCLA § 104(k); applicants addressing a single site
may request a waiver up to $650,000, which EPA says it expects to
approve only on an extremely limited basis. Recipients must provide a
20% cost share in money, labor, materials or services; EPA's
types-of-funding page says certain government entities may request a
hardship waiver. EPA requires cleanup applicants to publish a community
notification ad by January 7, 2027 and hold a public meeting by
January 14, 2027.

## Eligibility

Same entity list as assessment grants: EPA's eligible-entities page
(read 2026-11-04 via archived copy) includes a "General Purpose Unit
of Local Government" defined to include a "town." The pages read do
not state the threshold criteria for cleanup grants (e.g. site
ownership, prior assessment); those are in the FY2027 cleanup
guidelines (grants.gov 364202), which were not readable from this
workspace and must be checked before deciding.

**Verdict:** The Town of Kingston is eligible as an applicant type.
Whether it has a contaminated property that meets the cleanup
guidelines' threshold criteria needs confirmation.

## Fit

Narrow: only applies if the town controls a specific contaminated
parcel it wants remediated. Likely a second-step grant after an
assessment grant. Needs confirmation.
