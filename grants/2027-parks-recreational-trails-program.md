## Summary

Federal (FHWA) pass-through administered by OPRHP for maintenance and
restoration of existing trails, trailhead/trailside facilities and
linkages, new trail construction, trail-maintenance equipment, easement
or fee acquisition from willing sellers, and trail condition/
accessibility assessments, for both non-motorized and motorized use
(30/30/40 split by federal law). Applied for through the CFA; the Parks
grants page listed it as "Closed" on 2026-11-04 and the program page
references the 2025 RTP Guide. Historically offered through the annual
CFA; expect it in the 2027 CFA.

## Eligibility

2023 RTP CFA Guidance
(https://www.parks.ny.gov/documents/grants/RTPCFAGuidanceDocument2023.pdf):
eligible applicants are "Municipalities" and "Pre-Qualified
Not-for-Profit Corporations." Minimum federal request $25,000 ($31,250
total project) for trail work, $5,000 for equipment-only; maximum
$250,000. Federal funding "up to 80 percent of the total project cost."
The current program page itself does not restate eligibility; confirm
in the 2025 RTP Guide before applying.

**Verdict: Town of Kingston is eligible** as a municipality.

## Fit

A trail on town land, a trailhead parking area, or trail-maintenance
equipment for the highway/parks crew; the $5,000 equipment floor is
very accessible. Whether the town owns or manages any trail needs
confirmation.

## Effort

- CFA application with Cost Share Summary, SEQRA documentation, photos
  and maps; Landowner Permission Attestation for equipment; Notice to
  Owner letters for acquisitions.
- Documented property rights or agreement with the landowner.
- Federal-aid rules: no work before federal authorization and executed
  state contract; reimbursement up to 80%; monthly employment
  utilization reports for construction contracts over $10,000; right-of-
  way clearance certificate; equipment inventory.
- Authorizing resolution naming a Responsible Local Official.
