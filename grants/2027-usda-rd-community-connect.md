## Summary

Funds construction, acquisition, or leasing of broadband facilities and
equipment to bring service to the "least connected rural communities"
on a "community-oriented connectivity" basis. The applicant must
deliver at least 100/20 Mbps to all premises in the target community,
and the community must currently lack broadband service of 10/1 Mbps.
The FY2026 round (RDRUS-CC-2026, Assistance Listing 10.863) was posted
May 13, 2026 and closed June 29, 2026; it is an annual program, so a
FY2027 round is expected in roughly the same spring window. NACo's
summary of the FY2026 announcement
(https://naco.org/news/usda-announces-44-million-rural-broadband-connectivity-grant-opportunities-fy-2026)
puts it at $17 million alongside a $27 million Distance Learning &
Telemedicine round.

## Eligibility

Eligible applicants on the grants.gov listing (read 2026-11-04):
"Special district governments; City or township governments;
Federally recognized Native American tribal governments; County
governments; State governments," nonprofits with and without
501(c)(3), small businesses and other for-profits.

**Verdict:** A NY town is an eligible applicant. The harder tests are
on the service area, not the applicant: the proposed area must lack
10/1 Mbps service today and the applicant must build and deliver
100/20 Mbps service to every premise — i.e. the town would be the
broadband provider or partner with one. Whether any part of the Town
of Kingston still lacks 10/1 service needs confirmation against the
FCC and NY broadband maps.

## Fit

Plausible only if an unserved pocket remains and the town is willing
to own or partner on a network; needs confirmation. Minimum award is
$100,000, within reach, but the operating commitment is unusual for a
town with no utility department.

## Effort

Submitted through USDA RD's online system (not grants.gov) per the
NACo summary; the listing says "Late or incomplete applications will
not be accepted." Match required (amount in the NOFO/Application
Guide, not on the listing). Regulation is 7 CFR part 1739. Town-side
steps not stated in the notice: SAM.gov registration, a network design
and operating partner, a board resolution.
