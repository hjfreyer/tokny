## Summary

The Ulster County Transportation Council (the county's MPO) solicits
"concept-level transportation planning, analysis, and design" projects
from municipalities each fall for inclusion in its annual Unified
Planning Work Program. Example project types from the call:
transportation infrastructure plans (bicycle, pedestrian, complete
streets, traffic calming, resiliency planning); transportation services
and programs (transit, mobility management, micro-mobility);
transportation/land-use coordination (corridor or safety plans, zoning
updates); transportation data analysis. Funds cannot be used for
property acquisition, site preparation, existing staff, preliminary
engineering, detailed design, operations and management, or
construction.

The call states in capitals: "THIS IS NOT A GRANT PROGRAM." UCTC
administers and leads all approved projects; no funds go directly to
municipalities. It is filed here because it is the only recurring
county-level source of free transportation planning for a town, and a
UCTC-produced plan is a common prerequisite for later capital grants
(TAP, Complete Streets, Safe Streets for All).

## Eligibility

From the 2027 call: "Cities, towns, or villages in Ulster County" or a
Non-Voting UCTC Advisory Member Organization may apply. Not-for-profits,
neighborhood groups and others may apply through a municipal sponsor.
Multiple applications allowed if prioritized.

**Verdict:** The Town of Kingston is a town in Ulster County; eligible.

## Fit

A town with a state highway or county road running through it, a
hamlet center, or a flood-prone road segment could use a safety,
traffic-calming, or resiliency study. Whether the Town has any such
need or has previously used UCTC planning assistance needs confirmation.

## Effort

- Application form in the call PDF; hand deliver, postmark, or email to
  Brian C. Slack, UCTC, 244 Fair Street, PO Box 1800, Kingston, NY
  12402 (email listed as bsla@ulstercountyny.gov, and in one place as
  bsla@co.ulster.ny.us — confirm), by close of business Friday
  2026-12-11.
- "Applicants are strongly encouraged to contact UCTC staff before
  developing a proposal" — (845) 334-5590.
- Scored out of 100: summary (5), need/timing (15), alignment with the
  2050 LRTP or other UCTC plans (15), safety and community benefit (30),
  multimodal mobility (5), task outline (5), deliverables (10),
  additional funding / in-kind (5), elected-official support and
  outreach (10). A town board letter or resolution of support helps
  the last item.
- Selected projects are prioritized by staff resources, local need,
  and regional significance, then adopted through the draft UPWP
  process (public comment ~January–February).
