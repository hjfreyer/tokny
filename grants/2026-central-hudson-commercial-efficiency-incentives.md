## Summary

Central Hudson's commercial energy-efficiency program, administered by
ICF, offers (a) a **prescriptive** track with fixed incentive amounts
for replacing existing equipment (HVAC, and per the site navigation,
lighting and other measures), and (b) a **custom** track covering "a
wide range of efficiency upgrades, including new construction." Case
studies on the HVAC page include a school district ($900,000) and a
college (almost $1 million) as well as a small restaurant ($4,647 on a
$12,746 project), so public institutions have participated. No deadlines
or program end dates are listed.

## Eligibility

The page addresses "businesses" and lists no formal eligibility rules;
municipalities are not mentioned. Non-residential Central Hudson
accounts are the practical criterion (the `sources/central-hudson.md`
note that municipalities qualify as commercial customers is not
confirmed on the page). The commercial-lighting page
(https://www.CentralHudson.com/en/my-energy/save-energy-money/business-incentives/commercial-lighting/)
returned an error on 2026-11-04.

**Verdict:** Likely eligible as a non-residential customer, but the
page does not say so. Confirm by calling the program line.

## Fit

Town hall or highway garage lighting, heating/cooling, or
weatherization upgrades. Whether the Town's buildings are on Central
Hudson electric/gas accounts, and their condition, need confirmation.

## Effort

- "To get started, email CHGEPrograms@icf.com or call (800) 515-5353."
- Detailed rebate PDFs for prescriptive and custom measures are
  referenced on the page but not linked in the text read.
- Incentives are typically applied for before installation; any
  contract will need a board resolution.
