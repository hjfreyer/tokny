## Summary

Federal-aid program for "community-centered, active transportation
projects": on- and off-road pedestrian and bicycle facilities, safe
routes to school, rail-trail conversions, turnouts/overlooks, historic
transportation facility preservation, vegetation management,
environmental mitigation. The 2025 statewide solicitation ($97M)
opened Nov 12, 2025 and closed March 12, 2026. No new solicitation was
announced as of 2026-11-04; NYSDOT has run TAP solicitations roughly
every two years, so the next is anticipated but unscheduled.

## Eligibility

Governor's release 2025-11-12
(https://www.governor.ny.gov/news/governor-hochul-announces-97-million-available-funds-community-centered-active-transportation):
eligible applicants are "Local governments (counties, towns, cities,
villages)", transit agencies, natural resource/public land agencies,
tribal governments, and local/regional transportation or trail
entities; school districts, MPOs and NGOs only in partnership. Award
range $500,000–$7.0M; NYSDOT covers up to 80%, sponsor at least 20%.
Ulster County Transportation Council posted the solicitation
(https://uctc.ulstercountyny.gov/announcement/2025-statewide-tap-solicitation).

**Verdict: Town of Kingston is eligible**, but the $500,000 project
floor means a minimum ~$100,000 local match.

## Fit

Sidewalks or a shared-use path in a hamlet, or a safe route to a
school, would qualify; the floor is large for a 900-person town and
federal-aid delivery (Local Projects Manual) is burdensome. Needs
confirmation of any such corridor and of the town's appetite for a
federal-aid project.

## Effort

- NOFA, application, guidebook and FAQ are posted on the NYSDOT TAP
  page during a solicitation; workshops held statewide.
- Federal-aid local project process (design approval, ROW, NEPA,
  Davis-Bacon) — typically requires a consultant; coordinate with UCTC
  (the Ulster County MPO).
- Reimbursement; 20% local match committed by resolution.
