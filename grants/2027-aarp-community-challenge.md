## Summary

AARP's annual national "quick-action" grant for projects that make
communities more livable for all ages. Three grant types in the 2026
cycle: **Flagship** (public places, transportation and mobility,
housing, digital connections and literacy, disaster resilience);
**Capacity-Building Microgrants** ($2,500 plus webinars, cohort
learning and up to two hours of coaching, for walk audits, bike
audits, HomeFit modifications, disaster preparedness); **Demonstration**
(pedestrian safety, high-speed internet, housing design competitions;
must be replicable). Projects must be completed by December 15 of the
grant year; an after-action report is due December 31.

## Eligibility

Program page: 501(c)(3), 501(c)(4), and 501(c)(6) nonprofits recognized
by the IRS, **"Government entities,"** and other organizations
considered case by case; for-profits and individuals are not funded.
The 2026-01-07 press release invites "local eligible nonprofit
organizations and governments across the country."

**Verdict:** Towns are explicitly eligible. Qualifies.

## Fit

Small, fast money for a bench/shade/accessibility fix at a town park,
a walk audit of the hamlet, a crosswalk or wayfinding project, or an
emergency-preparedness training. Scoring is Impact 60 / Execution 30 /
Innovation 10, so a concrete, photographable project on town property
is the right shape. Needs confirmation of a candidate site.

## Effort

- Application through the AARP portal; late or incomplete applications
  are not reviewed.
- Selected grantees sign a grant agreement, complete eSupplier
  (vendor) registration, follow promotional requirements, collect
  signed releases for identifiable people in project photos, and file
  an after-action report.
- No match. A board resolution is not mentioned but the grant agreement
  will need a signatory.
