## Summary

Competitive CWSRF-funded grants for projects using EPA-designated green
stormwater infrastructure (bioretention, porous pavement, green roofs,
stream daylighting, etc.), energy efficiency or water efficiency
practices. 2026 round closed June 12, 2026; $16.8M to eight projects
announced Sept 21, 2026 (recipients included the Town of Schroeppel
at $900K and several villages). No future round is listed; the program
has run annually (2025 and 2026 RFAs both noticed in spring).

## Eligibility

Program page (read 2026-11-04) does not state applicant criteria; it
describes projects "across New York State". The April 2026 GIGP summary
(https://efc.ny.gov/system/files/documents/2026/04/04172026-gigp-summary.pdf)
was not read. The governor's release lists municipalities, a sewer
authority and a nonprofit as 2026 recipients, so towns have received
awards.

**Verdict: likely eligible (towns have been awarded), but eligibility
text and award floor must be read from the GIGP program summary before
pursuing.**

## Fit

A green-infrastructure retrofit of a town hall / highway garage parking
lot or a drainage problem area could fit, but 2026 awards were all
≥ $450K, suggesting projects larger than a 900-person town typically
runs. Needs confirmation of a candidate site.

## Effort

- Program summary, documentation guidance, feasibility and engineering
  report outlines are on the page; a feasibility/engineering report is
  expected with the application.
- Applications via EFC Customer Portal (portal.efc.ny.gov) in recent
  rounds.
