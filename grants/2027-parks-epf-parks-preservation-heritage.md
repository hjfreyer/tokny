## Summary

OPRHP's annual CFA grant with three tracks: **Parks** (acquire, develop
or plan parks and recreation facilities — playgrounds, courts, trails,
swimming/boating facilities; must reflect SCORP priorities), **Historic
Preservation** (acquire, rehabilitate or restore properties listed or
approved for listing on the State/National Register, plus structural
assessments and planning), and **Heritage Areas**. Matching,
reimbursement grants; 2026 deadline was 4:00 pm, July 31, 2026, via the
CFA. Expected again in the 2027 CFA.

## Eligibility

Program page (read 2026-11-04): "Funds are available to municipalities
or not-for-profits with an ownership interest." EPF FAQ
(https://regionalcouncils.ny.gov/sites/default/files/webinars/2026-06/Parks_Environmental_Protection_Fund_FAQ.pdf):
eligible applicants are "Municipalities, State agencies, public benefit
corporations, public authorities" and not-for-profits, with an ownership
interest (fee, easement, development rights or long-term lease). "Grant
awards are capped at $675,000"; grants cover up to 50% of approved
project cost, up to 75% in areas where "10 percent or more of the
population [is] below the poverty level."

**Verdict: Town of Kingston is eligible** as a municipality for any
park, recreation facility or Register-listed property it owns.

## Fit

A town park improvement, or rehabilitation of a State/National
Register-listed town hall or other town-owned historic building, is the
natural use. Whether the town owns a park or a listed building, and can
raise a 50% (or 25%) match, needs confirmation. Check the ZCTA poverty
table for the town's ZIP to see which match tier applies.

## Effort

- CFA application with Attachment Checklist items, Cost Share Summary,
  Gender-Based Violence certification; read the 2026 EPF Application
  Guidance Document and scoring criteria; email the Regional Grants
  Administrator for Ulster County.
- Parks grants require public access and parkland designation in
  perpetuity; preservation grants record a covenant/easement on the
  deed.
- Contract execution needs an authorizing resolution, certifications,
  and an Opinion of Municipal Counsel.
- Historic preservation work must follow the Secretary of the Interior's
  Standards.
