## Summary

Round 2 of a state capital grant for "building, renovating, and
modernizing community centers that promote physical health, mental
well-being, and connections among youth and older adults." Launched
Aug 31, 2026 with $75M; applications accepted through the DASNY Grants
Application Portal from Nov 2, 2026 until **3 p.m., Dec 7, 2026**;
awards no earlier than Feb 26, 2027. Priority for projects in
underserved communities.

## Eligibility

DASNY page (read 2026-11-04): grants are for "municipalities and
nonprofit organizations." Towns are not named individually. OPRHP's
grants page lists NY BRICKS as a "matching capital grant" open Nov 2 –
Dec 7, 2026.

**Verdict: Town of Kingston is likely eligible as a municipality**, but
read the RFA (linked on the page) for the full applicant definition and
match terms before relying on it.

## Fit

Only if the town has or wants a community/senior center it owns, with a
project of at least $250,000. Whether the town has such a facility, and
site control, needs confirmation. The floor is high for a 900-person
town but not impossible for a renovation.

## Effort

- RFA plus appendices on the page: Application Preparation Worksheet
  (App. 1), Program Specific Questions (App. 2), Certification (App. 3),
  Municipal Site Control Certification (App. 4), FAQ (App. 5), GDA
  Prerequisites (App. 7), Scoring Matrix (App. 8).
- Submission through DASNY Grants Application Portal by Dec 7, 2026.
