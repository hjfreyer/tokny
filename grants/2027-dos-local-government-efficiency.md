## Summary

Competitive reimbursement grants for projects that consolidate or share
services, or improve internal efficiency, among two or more local
governments. Eligible costs: legal and consultant services, capital
improvements and equipment, and transitional personnel costs up to three
years. Priority areas: IT including cybersecurity, emergency management/
services, shared code enforcement and planning, water and wastewater,
clean energy. 2025-26 round: $8M, RFAs due Feb 4, 2026; 25 awards
announced Oct 26, 2026. That release says "a new Request for
Applications is expected in the coming weeks," so the 2026-27 round is
anticipated with a deadline likely in early 2027.

## Eligibility

DOS press release 2026-10-26 (https://dos.ny.gov/node/92116): "Eligible
Local Government entities are counties, cities, towns, villages, special
improvement districts, fire districts, public libraries, association
libraries, public library systems..., water authorities, sewer
authorities, regional planning and development boards, school districts,
and Boards of Cooperative Educational Services." Dec 8, 2025
announcement: applications "must involve two or more local governments";
planning grants require a 50% match, implementation 10%.

**Verdict: Town of Kingston is eligible**, provided it applies with at
least one other local government (neighboring town, village, fire
district, county).

## Fit

Shared highway equipment, joint code enforcement, shared IT/
cybersecurity, or a shared assessor/clerk function with an adjacent town
are the usual small-town LGEG projects; planning grants at $20k/50% are
a low bar. Whether the town has a willing partner needs confirmation.

## Effort

- Wait for the 2026-27 RFA (expected Nov–Dec 2026); prior round
  guidance at
  https://dos.ny.gov/2025-26-request-applications-guidance-document-pdf.
- Multi-municipal application with each partner's resolution/letter;
  work plan, budget, ROI analysis; project must be completable within 5
  years.
- Evaluation: return on investment, need, service delivery benefits,
  operational changes, capacity, readiness, work plan/budget quality.
