## Summary

Central Hudson pays most of the electrical infrastructure cost ("make
ready") of installing Level 2 or DC fast chargers, under the PSC's
statewide EV make-ready order. Per the Participant Manual (updated
2024-01-12): the standard tier (up to 90%) covers "publicly accessible
L2 and DCFC sites with standardized plugs, including municipal
pay-to-park and free parking"; the maximum tier (up to 100%) covers
non-proprietary DCFC in Disadvantaged Communities, L2 at eligible
multi-unit dwellings, and curbside L2 in or adjacent to Disadvantaged
Communities; the reduced tier (50%) covers non-public sites.
Applications are accepted "until port goals (2,037 L2 and 416 DCFC)
are met or the $44,897,460 in funding is allocated, whichever comes
first." 20% of budget is earmarked for EJ/LMI zones.

## Eligibility

Participant Manual: participants can be "Central Hudson customers,
developers, equipment owners, site hosts, or Approved Contractors";
"public and private entities, both for-profit and not-for-profit, may
participate." Municipalities are not a separate category but
municipality-operated lots are explicitly named as publicly accessible
(90% tier). Ulster County's own SEVI grant page described the Central
Hudson program as the companion to municipal EV projects.

**Verdict:** A town that is a Central Hudson electric customer with a
public parking lot (town hall, park) is eligible. Whether the Town is in
Central Hudson's electric territory and whether it is or borders a
Disadvantaged Community need confirmation.

## Fit

Only worthwhile if the Town wants public chargers at town hall or a
park. The program does not pay for the chargers; a second funding source
(NYSERDA Charge Ready / state Municipal ZEV rebates, or a county round)
is usually needed for the equipment. Needs confirmation of interest.

## Effort

- Apply through the online portal ch-evmakeready.programprocessing.com
  or email a printable application to EVMakeReadyCH@ICF.com; an
  Approved Contractor can apply on the Town's behalf.
- Required: project description, quotes or invoices, site plan,
  satellite image, charger specifications, signed terms.
- Incentive is paid after installation; a board resolution will be
  needed for the installation contract.
- Questions: EVMakeready@cenhud.com.
