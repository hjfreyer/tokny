## Summary

Small store-level grants from Walmart facilities to organizations
serving the same community. Three application cycles per Walmart fiscal
year (FY runs February 1 – January 31). Guidelines page lists the FY27
cycles as Cycle 1: February 1 – April 15; Cycle 2: May 1 – July 15;
Cycle 3: August 1 – December 31, but also says "the last day to submit
is November 30" — the page is internally inconsistent; confirm before
relying on December. One grant per facility per fiscal year; up to 25
pending/approved applications at once.

## Eligibility

Guidelines page: eligible types include 501(c)(3) public charities,
schools, faith-based groups with community-wide projects, and
**government entities**, listed as "U.S. federal agencies, territories
and commonwealths, state agencies and departments" and "political
subdivisions (including cities, counties, municipal agencies)."
501(c)(4), (c)(6) and (c)(19) organizations are not eligible.
Organizations "must serve the same service area as the facility they
request funding from" and must have a verified Spark Good account on
Walmart.com (verification by Deed).

**Verdict:** A town government is a political subdivision and is
eligible. Requires a Walmart facility whose service area includes the
Town; whether the nearest Walmart (Kingston area) treats the Town of
Kingston as in its service area needs confirmation.

## Fit

Small purchases: park equipment, emergency-services supplies, a
community event, youth/senior program materials. Low effort, low
amount.

## Effort

- Create and verify a Spark Good account at walmart.com/nonprofits
  (Deed verification); only authorized account users may apply.
- Apply via the "Apply today" link on the guidelines page during an
  open cycle; the local store manager reviews.
- No resolution or match mentioned.
