## Summary

SAFER helps fire departments increase frontline firefighters through
two activities: (1) hiring firefighters and (2) recruiting and
retaining volunteer firefighters. The second track is the one that
matters for a volunteer department — it funds recruitment campaigns,
retention incentives, training, and related costs. The FY2025 round
(DHS-25-GPD-083-00-99) opened May 19, 2026 and closed June 22, 2026
alongside AFG and FP&S ($324M total). The grants.gov listing shows no
cost sharing. Annual program; next round expected spring 2027 subject
to appropriation.

## Eligibility

grants.gov listing (read 2026-11-04): "City or township governments,"
county governments, tribal governments, 501(c)(3) nonprofits, "Other,"
with the note "Fire Departments, National, state, local, or federally
recognized tribal organizations representing the interests of
volunteer firefighters."

**Verdict:** Eligible applicant type, but as with AFG the real
applicant is the fire department. Whether the Town of Kingston runs a
department, or an independent fire district / company does, needs
confirmation.

## Fit

Good in kind for any volunteer department struggling with membership.
Fit for the town itself hinges on who operates fire protection — needs
confirmation.

## Effort

FEMA GO application in the same window as AFG. Needs SAM.gov/UEI and
a recruitment/retention project narrative. No match per the listing.
