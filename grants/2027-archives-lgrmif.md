## Summary

Annual grant to local governments for records management projects in
five categories: Disaster Management, Document Conversion and Access
(scanning/digitization), Files Management, Historical Records, and
Inactive Records (storage/shelving). 2026-27 cycle: portal opened Feb
17, 2026; applications due 5:00 pm, April 1, 2026; grant period July 1,
2026 – June 30, 2027. The program runs every year on the same calendar;
the 2027-28 deadline is expected to be April 1, 2027 (not yet posted —
the deadlines page lists only 2026-27 dates).

## Eligibility

LGRMIF page (read 2026-11-04): the program "provides grants to local
governments." NYSED RFP #264 page lists examples ("Local Authorities,
Sponsored Community Colleges, Fire Districts, Volunteer Fire Companies")
and refers to the RFP's Eligibility Requirements section. Towns are not
named on either page, but the program is for local governments broadly
and the Town of Hurley (Ulster County) posted a 2026 resolution to apply
(townofhurleyny.gov), which suggests towns apply routinely.

**Verdict: Town of Kingston is eligible as a local government** (confirm
against the RFP eligibility section when the 2027-28 guidelines post).

## Fit

Classic town-clerk grant: scanning minutes and deeds, records shelving,
or a disaster plan for vital records; no match stated. Needs
confirmation of the clerk's priorities and that the town has a
designated Records Management Officer with an eGrants account.

## Effort

- The RMO needs an eGrants user account (new-account registration
  deadline was March 18, 2026 for the last cycle — about two weeks
  before the application deadline).
- Electronic application in eGrants; paper signature forms postmarked
  by the deadline; MWBE documents due unless deferred.
- Optional "Get Ready for LGRMIF" workshops.
- Town of Hurley's 2026 resolution indicates a board resolution
  authorizing the application is customary.
