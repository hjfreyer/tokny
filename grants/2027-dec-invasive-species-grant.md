## Summary

DEC's recurring grant for invasive species work. Round 4 categories:
(1) aquatic invasive species spread prevention / boat decontamination
(watercraft stewards, decon equipment); (2) terrestrial and aquatic
invasive species rapid response and control (mechanical removal,
chemical treatment, biocontrol); (3) research; (4) restoration and
resiliency of sites impacted by invasive species. Round 4 bids were due
Dec 17, 2025; awards (>$5.1M, 51 projects) announced March 2026. The page
says "This grant is now closed" and does not state when the next round
opens. Previous rounds have been announced in the fall (Round 4 RFA was
announced Oct 2025).

## Eligibility

Program page (read 2026-11-04): eligible applicants are
"Municipalities, academic institutions, and 501(c)(3) not-for-profit
corporations whose projects are within the eligible geographic
boundaries." Unincorporated groups (e.g. lake associations) must apply
through one of those as lead. Round 4 required a 25% match ("a $50,000
grant needs $12,500 in match"). Up to two bids per bidder.

**Verdict: Town of Kingston is eligible** as a municipality (towns not
named individually, but "municipalities" is the applicant class).

## Fit

Control of invasives on town land or road rights-of-way, or restoration
of an infested town parcel. Whether the town has a specific infestation
or site needs confirmation; award size is not stated on the page so
check the Round 4 RFA for floors.

## Effort

- Round 4 was a bid through the RFA process; read the Round 4 RFA (PDF
  linked on page) for bid forms, eligible expenditures and evaluation
  criteria. Expect SFS Grants Management submission in the next round.
- 25% match.
