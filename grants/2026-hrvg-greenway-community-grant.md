## Summary

Matching planning grants from the Hudson River Valley Greenway (HRVG) for
municipalities that have designated themselves "Greenway Communities".
Eligible project types per the March 2026 guidelines
(https://hudsongreenway.ny.gov/system/files/documents/2026/03/greenway-community-grant-guidelines.pdf):
comprehensive or master planning; zoning and subdivision ordinance
updates; natural resource inventories and protection plans; open space
inventories and conservation strategies; cultural and historic resource
planning; waterfront revitalization planning along the Hudson and its
tributaries. Project categories on the program page: community planning,
public participation, open space protection, cultural resource
protection, natural resource protection, economic development, scenic
resource protection. Projects involving public infrastructure must comply
with the Community Risk and Resiliency Act (CRRA).

A separate, larger **Compact Community Grant** tier ($50,000 municipal /
$110,000 intermunicipal) exists only for municipalities in counties that
have adopted a Greenway Compact Plan. The program page lists the Compact
counties as Westchester, Rockland, Putnam, Orange and Dutchess; Ulster is
not listed, so the Compact tier is not available to the Town of Kingston
unless Ulster County adopts a Compact Plan.

## Eligibility

From the March 2026 guidelines, a municipality must:

- "Be located within the Greenway Area; and"
- "Adopt a local board resolution designating the municipality as a Greenway Community; and"
- "Complete all previously awarded Greenway Community Grant projects prior to submitting a new application."

The Greenway Area page (https://hudsongreenway.ny.gov/greenway-area-map)
lists Ulster County as one of the 14 Greenway counties, and notes that a
2006 change added the Ulster County portion of the Catskill Park. The
February 2026 flyer says 287 of 323 eligible municipalities have opted
in. Becoming a Greenway Community is by board resolution; "After the
Greenway receives the resolution and the Board approves it, the
municipality is recognized as a Greenway Community."

**Verdict:** A New York town is the intended applicant. The Town of
Kingston is in a Greenway county, so it is eligible once it has (or if it
already has) adopted a Greenway Community resolution. Whether the Town
has already done so needs confirmation (no list of designated
communities was found on the site; the Greenway map page shows the
category legend but does not assign named municipalities).

## Fit

Pays for exactly the planning work a small town typically cannot fund
(comp plan update, zoning, natural resource inventory). Whether the Town
has a current comprehensive plan or any pending planning need, and
whether it is already a Greenway Community, needs confirmation.

## Effort

- Board resolution designating the Town a Greenway Community (if not
  already done) — must be received and approved by the Greenway board
  before applying.
- Notarized municipal resolution supporting the project and committing
  the local match, due with the application.
- Grant Application Budget form; Match Worksheet.
- Application through the online portal at hrvg.grantplatform.com
  (portal page could not be read on 2026-11-04).
- During contract: progress reports every six months; reimbursement
  on itemized invoices and proof of payment; any plan produced must be
  formally adopted by the town board before final payment.
- Program page says to contact the Greenway *before* the round opens;
  "Project specific questions will not be answered while applications
  are open." Contacts on the 2026 flyer: Cody Arana
  (Cody.Arana@hudsongreenway.ny.gov), Dan Jeanson
  (Daniel.Jeanson@hudsongreenway.ny.gov); program line 518-473-3835,
  hrvg@hudsongreenway.ny.gov.

## Notes

- 2026-11-04: Program page lists round dates without a year:
  "Application opens: October 5; Application closes: November 6; Grants
  awarded: December." If that is the 2026 round it closes in two days;
  the 2025 round (announced 2025-09-10,
  https://hudsongreenway.ny.gov/news/hudson-river-valley-greenway-announces-100000-available-community-grants)
  had a 2025-10-31 deadline. Treat as **annual, fall window**; verify the
  current year's dates on the program page or portal each September.
- 2026-11-04: The 2025 announcement listed the intermunicipal cap as
  $30,000; the current program page and the March 2026 guidelines say
  $45,000. Use the current guidelines.
- 2026-11-04: Compact tier not available (Ulster not a Compact County).
