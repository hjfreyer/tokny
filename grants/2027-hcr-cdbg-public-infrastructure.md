## Summary

The Small Cities CDBG infrastructure track for non-entitlement
communities: water, sewer and stormwater projects only (public
facilities, planning, housing and imminent threat are separate RFAs).
2026 round: ~$25M, opened May 18, 2026, due 4:00 pm Friday, July 31,
2026 via the CFA; rolling awards until exhausted. Runs annually with
the CFA; expect the 2027 RFA in May 2027.

## Eligibility

2026 RFA (read 2026-11-04): "Eligible applicants are non-entitlement
units of general local government (county, town, city, or village)."
Non-entitlement cities, towns and villages are those under 50,000
population (except MSA principal cities). Activities must meet a HUD
national objective; for public infrastructure "at least 51% of
beneficiaries are LMI" (documented by census data if the service area
matches census geography, otherwise an income survey). Joint
applications allowed for shared systems after contacting OCR.

**Verdict: Town of Kingston is eligible** as a non-entitlement town;
the binding constraint is proving ≥51% low/moderate-income beneficiaries
in the service area.

## Fit

Only applies if the town has (or is creating) a water, sewer or
stormwater system serving a primarily residential, majority-LMI area —
needs confirmation. For a rural town without municipal utilities this
is a pass; for drainage work in a hamlet it could fit.

## Effort

- Public hearing before a quorum of the full town board before applying
  (model materials provided).
- UEI from SAM.gov.
- Draft Environmental Review Record (NEPA + SEQRA) recommended at
  application.
- Income survey if census geography doesn't match the service area.
- For the $2M tier: firm co-funding commitments (USDA Preliminary
  Funding Estimate, EFC Letter of Eligibility/Hardship Determination).
- Davis-Bacon on construction over $2,000; Section 3 over $200,000.
- Technical assistance: HCR_CFA@hcr.ny.gov, 518-474-2057.
