**Certified Local Government** program: towns that adopt a historic
preservation law and commission and get certified by the NY SHPO become
eligible for an annual spring CLG subgrant round (surveys, National
Register nominations, design guidelines, training). Filed as
`2027-nps-certified-local-government`. Whether the Town of Kingston has
a preservation law or CLG status needs confirmation; if not, the first
step is a multi-month local process, not an application.

LWCF stateside money reaches towns through the NY Parks EPF round in
the CFA, not through NPS directly.
