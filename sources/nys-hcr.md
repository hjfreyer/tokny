Housing and CDBG. Two things matter for a small town:

- **CDBG** (non-entitlement communities, i.e. towns like this one):
  Public Infrastructure (CFA, July; water/sewer/stormwater, up to
  $1.5–2M, 51% low/moderate-income test) — `2027-hcr-cdbg-public-infrastructure`;
  and the rolling Open Round (Community Planning $50k with no match;
  Public Facilities / Imminent Threat $1M) — `2026-hcr-cdbg-open-round`.
- **Pro-Housing Communities certification** — not a grant, but it now
  gates the Regional Council Capital Fund, NY Main Street, EFC Clean
  Water for Housing and the Mid-Hudson Momentum Fund. Filed as
  `2026-hcr-pro-housing-communities-certification`. Whether the Town of
  Kingston is certified needs confirmation; if not, it is a board
  decision worth having.

## Rejected

- NY Main Street — needs Pro-Housing certification and a deteriorated
  mixed-use corridor with >50% low-income residents.
- Resilient Homes & Communities RISC — Ulster not an eligible county.
- ESD Pro-Housing Supply Funds — $2.5M+ / 20+ units; out of scale.
