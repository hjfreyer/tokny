Annual quick-action grant program; "government entities" are explicitly
eligible, no match. Flagship grants up to ~$15k, $2.5k microgrants,
demonstration grants up to $25k, for small projects that make a place
more livable for all ages: benches, crosswalks, park fixes, wayfinding,
community events.

Window: opens January, closes early March (2026 round closed
2026-03-04). Filed as `2027-aarp-community-challenge`.
