Only one program relevant to a town as applicant: **Emergency Watershed
Protection**, where a town can be the sponsor for post-storm work
(debris-clogged culverts and streams, bank erosion threatening roads).
Event-driven, no disaster declaration needed. Filed as
`2027-usda-nrcs-emergency-watershed-protection`. Contact is the NY
state conservationist's office / local NRCS field office after a storm.
