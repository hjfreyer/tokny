Planning and local-government money. Most of it runs through the CFA;
Local Government Efficiency has its own RFA.

## Programs filed

- Local Government Efficiency (new RFA "expected in coming weeks" as of
  late Oct 2026; last due Feb 2026) — `2027-dos-local-government-efficiency`.
  Needs two or more governments; planning $20k / implementation $250k.
- Smart Growth Community Planning (CFA) — `2027-dos-smart-growth-community-planning`.
  Comprehensive plan / zoning; $75k minimum.
- Local Waterfront Revitalization (CFA) — `2027-dos-local-waterfront-revitalization`.
  Only for municipalities along a designated inland waterway. DOS's
  list includes the Esopus, Rondout, Wallkill and **Sawkill Creek
  (Ulster County)**; whether any of these runs through the Town of
  Kingston needs confirmation — if so, LWRP and a $2M set-aside in
  Smart Growth both open up.

## Rejected

- Brownfield Opportunity Area — needs identifiable brownfield sites and
  is really a county/multi-jurisdiction program.
- Countywide Shared Services — county-led; towns join the county plan
  (earns priority points on LGE applications).
