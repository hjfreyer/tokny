State power authority. Not a grant-maker in the usual sense; it finances
and delivers municipal energy projects and is repaid from savings.

## Programs

- **Smart Street Lighting NY** — turnkey LED streetlight conversion,
  financed by NYPA and repaid from energy savings; includes the Public
  Service Law §70-a path for buying utility-owned lights. A Smart City
  Technology Grant was attached to it (2021 announcement: up to $500k;
  current amounts not on the page). Filed as
  `2027-nypa-smart-street-lighting-ny`.
- Energy services for local governments — audits, retrofits, solar,
  EV fleets, all on the same finance-and-repay model.
