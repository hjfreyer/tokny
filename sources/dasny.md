Mostly a conduit for legislatively directed capital grants, but it
also runs occasional open programs. Filed: **NY BRICKS** community
center program, $250k–$15M, "municipalities and nonprofits", opens
Nov 2, due **2026-12-07** — `2026-dasny-ny-bricks`. The floor is high
for a town this size unless there is a community-center project already
in mind.

Also the place to look if a state legislator offers a member-item
capital grant; those are administered here.
