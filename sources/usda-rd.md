The federal agency most likely to fund a 900-person town directly.
Most programs are rolling (apply any time through the NY state office
in Syracuse) and are loan/grant combinations where the grant share
depends on the service area's **median household income** relative to
the state nonmetro median — so that MHI figure is the first town fact
to pin down.

## Programs filed

- Community Facilities Direct Loan & Grant (town hall, highway garage,
  fire/EMS, equipment) — `2027-usda-rd-community-facilities`
- Water & Waste Disposal — `2027-usda-rd-water-waste-disposal`
- SEARCH predevelopment planning grants — `2027-usda-rd-search-grant`
- Emergency Community Water Assistance — `2027-usda-rd-emergency-community-water`
- Community Connect broadband (annual, closes ~June) — `2027-usda-rd-community-connect`
- Rural Business Development Grants (annual, closes ~June) — `2027-usda-rd-rural-business-development`

## Rejected

- REAP — ag producers and rural small businesses only.
- ReConnect — towns eligible but no round since 2024; re-file if one posts.
- Distance Learning & Telemedicine — needs end-user sites.

## Quirks

- rd.usda.gov program pages sometimes refuse automated fetches; the
  state office or a web search for the fact-sheet PDF works.
- Grants.gov eligibility code for these appears as "City or township
  governments"; USDA's own definition of "rural" for CF is ≤20,000
  population and for water programs ≤10,000.
