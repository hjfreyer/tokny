Senators Schumer and Gillibrand and the House member (NY-18, Pat Ryan —
confirm the town is in NY-18) each take Community Project Funding /
Congressionally Directed Spending requests from local governments every
spring for the next fiscal year. Nine appropriations bills accept them;
typical town asks are water/sewer, roads, town hall, fire/EMS, parks.
FY27 deadlines were mid/late March 2026; expect the FY28 cycle to open
around February–March 2027. Filed as
`2027-congress-directed-spending-earmarks`.

Not a competitive grant: success depends on the member's priorities and
on having a shovel-ready, well-documented project with a clear federal
nexus. Each office publishes its own form and rules each cycle.
