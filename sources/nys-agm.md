Farmland protection. The sequence matters: a town first writes a
**municipal farmland protection plan** (planning grant, $25k max, 20%
cash match, rolling under RFA 0263 — only for towns that have never
prepared one), and only then can it apply directly for
**implementation** (easement purchases up to $3M, rolling Round 20);
without a plan the route is through the county or a land trust.

Filed: `2026-agm-municipal-farmland-protection-planning`,
`2026-agm-farmland-protection-implementation`. Whether the town has a
farmland plan or active farms worth protecting needs confirmation.
