Road and bridge money. CHIPS / PAVE-NY / Extreme Winter Recovery are
**formula aid** the town already receives, filed only as a marker
(`2026-dot-chips-pave-ny-formula`). The competitive programs:

- BRIDGE NY — culverts and bridges; SFY26-27 budget $200M, solicitation
  anticipated. Official page blocked; filed from secondary sources,
  flagged — `2027-dot-bridge-ny`.
- Transportation Alternatives — sidewalks, trails, safe routes; $500k
  floor, 20% match, roughly biennial — `2027-dot-transportation-alternatives`.

## Quirks

- dot.ny.gov program pages (BRIDGENY, TAP, CHIPS) refuse automated
  reads. The NY Association of Towns and county highway superintendents'
  guides carry the same details and are readable.
