One application, many state programs. Each year the Regional Economic
Development Councils run a single round where a municipality submits
one CFA and selects which programs it is applying to.

Kingston is in the **Mid-Hudson** REDC region.

## 2026 round (confirmed 2026-11-03)

- Announced/opened **June 1, 2026**; deadline **July 31, 2026, 4 p.m.**
  for deadline-driven programs. Some programs are "open enrollment" and
  accept applications until funds run out — check which.
- 20+ programs from 8 agencies/authorities; up to $60M Regional Council
  Capital Funds plus each program's own pot.
- Authoritative program list: the CFA Available Resources manual,
  https://regionalcouncils.ny.gov/sites/default/files/2026-06/CFA_Available_Resources_2026.pdf
  (expect the 2027 edition at a similar path next June). Also the
  REDC Guidebook at https://regionalcouncils.ny.gov/file/2026-redc-guidebook.

The 2026 round is closed; next action is spring 2027.

## Prerequisites to apply

CFA account tied to the town; a board resolution authorizing the
application is usually required and must be scheduled ahead of the
deadline. Most programs require a local match (often 25–50%, in-kind
sometimes allowed).

## Quirks

- The window is short and predictable. Build the candidate list in
  spring from the resources manual, get board authorization in June,
  submit in July.
- Scoring favors projects already in a town comprehensive plan or
  climate action plan. Note that in `fit` assessments.
- Many of these programs also appear in SFS (see `sources/ny-sfs-grants.md`).
  Cross-reference before filing duplicates.
