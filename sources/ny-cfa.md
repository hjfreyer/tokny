One application, many state programs. Each year (typically opening in
**May** and closing late **July**) the Regional Economic Development
Councils run a single round where a municipality submits one CFA and
selects which programs it is applying to. Dozens of programs from DEC,
EFC, DOS, Parks, ESD, HCR, Ag & Markets, NYSERDA and others participate.

Kingston is in the **Mid-Hudson** REDC region.

## How to search

- Program list and the annual "Available Resources" guide are published
  at https://regionalcouncils.ny.gov each spring. Read the guide; it is
  the authoritative list of what is in the round.
- The CFA application itself is at the URL above (login required).

## Narrowing

The resources guide marks eligible applicant types per program. Look for
"municipalities" / "local governments". Historically relevant for a small
town:

- DEC Climate Smart Communities (implementation grants)
- EFC Green Innovation Grant Program, Engineering Planning Grants
- DOS Local Waterfront Revitalization, Smart Growth, Local Government
  Efficiency
- Parks EPF municipal parks and historic preservation
- Ag & Markets Farmland Protection Implementation
- NYSERDA Clean Energy Communities (sometimes inside CFA, sometimes
  standalone — check both)

## Prerequisites to apply

CFA account tied to the town; a board resolution authorizing the
application is usually required and must be scheduled ahead of the
deadline. Most programs require a local match (often 25–50%, in-kind
sometimes allowed).

## Quirks

- The window is short and predictable. Build the candidate list in
  spring from the resources guide, get board authorization in June,
  submit in July.
- Scoring favors projects already in a town comprehensive plan or
  climate action plan. Note that in `fit` assessments.
