$250–$5,000 grants from individual stores; "political subdivisions
(including cities, counties, municipal agencies)" are eligible. Quarterly
cycles. Requires a verified Spark Good account (a human has to create it)
and a store whose service area covers the town. Filed as
`2026-walmart-spark-good-local-grant`.

Rejected alongside it: **T-Mobile Hometown Grants** (towns under 50k, up
to $50k) — program announced its "last 25 towns" in June 2026 and runs
only through 2026. Re-check in 2027 in case it is renewed. Hudson Valley
foundations (Community Foundations of the Hudson Valley, Dyson, Klock
Kingston, NoVo) are nonprofit-only or closed to unsolicited requests;
Dyson makes an exception for municipal consolidation/merger studies.
