The state agency with the most town-sized programs. Two routes: the
annual CFA (Climate Smart Communities, WQIP) and standalone RFAs posted
in SFS (Hudson River Estuary Program, invasive species, forestry).

## Programs filed

- Climate Smart Communities grants (CFA, July) — `2027-dec-climate-smart-communities`.
  CSC registration is **not** required to apply; 50/50 match.
- Water Quality Improvement Project (CFA; Round 23 expected May 2027) —
  `2027-dec-wqip`. Salt storage, road-salt reduction, culverts; 25% match.
- Hudson River Estuary Program — Local Stewardship Planning and
  Tributary Restoration RFAs, both due **2026-12-17**;
  `2026-dec-hudson-estuary-local-stewardship-planning`,
  `2026-dec-hudson-estuary-tributary-restoration`. The latter funds
  **road culvert design and replacement** with a 10% match and names
  towns verbatim; needs a NAACC culvert assessment first (Ulster SWCD
  trains on these, see `sources/ulster-county.md`).
- Trees for Tribs (in-kind plantings) — `2027-dec-trees-for-tribs`.
- Invasive species grant (annual, December) — `2027-dec-invasive-species-grant`.

## Rejected (2026-11-04)

- Urban & Community Forestry — $50k minimum, tree inventories; not sized
  for a town with few street trees. Community Reforestation uses an
  "urban populations" eligibility map.
- Hudson Estuary River Access / River Education — town is not on the
  tidal Hudson (needs confirmation); education track not applicable.

## Quirks

- dec.ny.gov RFA PDFs under /media/ often refuse automated reads; the
  Environmental Notice Bulletin (ENB) carries the same notices.
- DEC's CSC page and the ENB disagree on the round number for the 2026
  deadline (10 vs 9); use the RFA number, not the round.
