The electric/gas utility serving the town. Not grants in the usual
sense, but utility programs frequently cover most of the cost of
municipal projects: streetlight LED conversion, building energy audits
and retrofits, EV charger make-ready, heat pumps for municipal
buildings.

## Where to look

- Business / commercial energy efficiency programs (municipalities
  qualify as commercial customers).
- Municipal streetlight purchase/conversion program — many NY towns have
  bought their streetlights from the utility and converted to LED using
  a combination of utility incentive and NYPA financing.
- Make-Ready (EV charging infrastructure) program.
- Community / charitable giving — small, but exists.

## Quirks

- Also check **NYPA** (New York Power Authority) for the streetlight
  and municipal efficiency financing side; NYPA + Central Hudson + NYSERDA
  CEC points often stack on the same project.
- Programs change with each rate case; verify current offerings rather
  than relying on an old file.
