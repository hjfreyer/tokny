The electric/gas utility serving the town. Not grants in the usual
sense, but utility programs frequently cover most of the cost of
municipal projects: streetlight LED conversion, building energy audits
and retrofits, EV charger make-ready, heat pumps for municipal
buildings.

## Where to look (checked 2026-11-04)

- **EV Make-Ready** — 90% of make-ready cost for public sites, 100% in
  disadvantaged communities; municipal lots explicitly named. Filed as
  `2026-central-hudson-ev-make-ready` from the Jan 2024 participant
  manual on centralhudson.com.
- **Commercial energy efficiency incentives** (run by ICF,
  CHGEPrograms@icf.com, 800-515-5353) — prescriptive and custom
  lighting/HVAC incentives; municipalities not named but school
  districts appear in case studies. Filed as
  `2026-central-hudson-commercial-efficiency-incentives`.
- **Streetlights** — only a 2015 LED lease program and the Public
  Service Law §70-a purchase path found; no current incentive page.
  The real program is NYPA's (see `sources/nypa.md`).
- **Nonprofit Sponsorships** — page title says nonprofit-only; page
  could not be read. Treat as unavailable until read by hand.

## Quirks

- `cenhud.com` blocks automated reads; the older `centralhudson.com`
  host served program PDFs. Verify by hand before relying on figures.
- Also check **NYPA** (New York Power Authority) for the streetlight
  and municipal efficiency financing side; NYPA + Central Hudson + NYSERDA
  CEC points often stack on the same project.
- Programs change with each rate case; verify current offerings rather
  than relying on an old file.
