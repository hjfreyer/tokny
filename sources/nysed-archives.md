The grant every NY town is eligible for and most small ones never apply
to. LGRMIF funds records inventories, digitization, shelving, disaster
planning, and records-management software; $75k individual / $150k
shared-services, no match stated. Annual, deadline around April 1.
The town's Records Management Officer (usually the clerk) must have an
eGrants account set up ~2 weeks before the deadline. Filed as
`2027-archives-lgrmif`.
