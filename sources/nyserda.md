State energy authority.

## Clean Energy Communities — status: CLOSED (checked 2026-11-03)

The program page (https://www.nyserda.ny.gov/All-Programs/Clean-Energy-Communities/How-It-Works)
now says the program is closed and no longer accepts high-impact-action
submissions toward the 1–5 star designation. Round 3.0 worked like this:
complete four high-impact actions → designated → earn points → grants
sized by star tier. That is over.

Signs of a redesign in 2026: an RFI 6113 "Clean Energy Communities —
Action Track" and an RFP 6120 "Technical Support Contractor: Cohort
Track" were issued. Watch the program page and the Mid-Hudson regional
coordinator for a relaunch; if it comes back it will likely be the
most town-sized grant in the state again.

## What is open

Program Opportunity Notices (PONs):
https://www.nyserda.ny.gov/Funding-Opportunities — filter for
municipalities / local governments. Things to look at:

- Building Cleaner Communities Competition (in the CFA; see
  `sources/ny-cfa.md`)
- FlexTech energy studies for municipal buildings (often no cost)
- EV charging: Charge Ready NY and utility make-ready (see
  `sources/central-hudson.md`)
- Clean heating/cooling incentives for municipal buildings
- Community solar / solar on municipal property

## Prerequisites to apply

Varies by PON. Most require a board resolution for anything with a
contract.
