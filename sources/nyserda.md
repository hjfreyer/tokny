State energy authority. Runs the **Clean Energy Communities** program,
which is probably the single most town-sized grant in New York: a
municipality completes "high-impact actions" (benchmarking, LED
streetlights, EV charging, clean energy codes, a climate smart task
force, etc.) and earns points redeemable for grants, with extra funding
for disadvantaged communities. Thousands in grant money is available for
actions that are mostly paperwork.

## How to search

- Clean Energy Communities: https://www.nyserda.ny.gov/All-Programs/Clean-Energy-Communities
  — check the action list, point values, and current grant tiers.
- Program Opportunity Notices (PONs) for everything else:
  https://www.nyserda.ny.gov/Funding-Opportunities (filter for
  municipalities / local governments).

## Narrowing

Clean Energy Communities first. Then look at: municipal building
efficiency (FlexTech studies are often free), EV charging (Charge Ready
NY), community solar / solar for municipal buildings, resilience.

## Prerequisites to apply

CEC requires registering the town and naming a point of contact; many
actions require a board resolution. There are regional coordinators who
will walk a town through it for free — find the Mid-Hudson one.

## Quirks

- CEC action list and point values change roughly annually. Snapshot the
  list into the grant file when assessing.
