State agency running three small annual grant programs for Hudson
Valley municipalities. The Town of Kingston is inside the Greenway Area
(Ulster is a Greenway county) but Ulster is **not** a Greenway Compact
county, so the larger Compact grants are unavailable.

## Programs

- **McHenry Community Planning Grant** — up to $20k municipal / $45k
  intermunicipal, dollar-for-dollar match (state funds can't be match),
  planning only: comprehensive plans, zoning, natural resource and open
  space inventories, cultural/historic resource plans, waterfront
  planning. Window listed as opens Oct 5 / closes Nov 6 (annual).
  Guidelines: https://hudsongreenway.ny.gov/system/files/documents/2026/03/greenway-community-grant-guidelines.pdf
- **Conservancy Trail Grant** — feasibility $50k / design $75k /
  construction $250k for locally owned trails. Jan 4 – Feb 12.
- **Heritage Development Grant** — up to $7,500; municipalities
  eligible if inside the National Heritage Area boundary.

## Prerequisites

The Community Grant requires the town to have adopted a board resolution
designating itself a **Greenway Community** and to have closed out any
prior Greenway grant. No public roster of designated communities was
found; whether the Town of Kingston is one needs confirmation (ask the
clerk, or HRVG at 518-473-3835 / hrvg@hudsongreenway.ny.gov).

## Quirks

- Application platform hrvg.grantplatform.com blocks automated reads,
  and the program pages print dates without a year. Confirm the year
  by phone.
- The Greenway Compact grant is for Westchester, Rockland, Putnam,
  Orange and Dutchess only. Rejected.
