County-level pass-throughs and small programs. No single portal.

## Where to look (checked 2026-11-04)

- **participate.ulstercountyny.gov** — where county grant rounds
  actually get posted (SEVI solar/EV grants, parks grants, Green the
  Scene, Housing Action Fund). Check this first.
- **Ulster County Transportation Council (UCTC)** — annual November
  "Call for Planning Projects"; towns eligible. Not cash to the town:
  UCTC does or contracts the planning work (~$200k/yr federal planning
  funds). Filed as `2026-uctc-call-for-planning-projects`.
- **Ulster County Planning Department** — no standing municipal grant
  program found. Runs the Housing Smart Communities Initiative (the
  Town of Kingston is already listed as participating) which offers
  technical support and "access to funding" by certification tier, but
  publishes no grant terms.
- **Ulster County Dept of Environment** — Climate Smart task force and
  monthly municipal committee; technical assistance and funding
  announcements, no county grant program of its own.
- **Ulster County Tourism** — Event Marketing Support Fund (up to $3k,
  annual, March deadline). 2026 eligibility text names organizations,
  nonprofits and businesses, not municipalities; worth one call to
  845-340-3566 since towns host community days.
- **Ulster County SWCD** (845-883-7162 x3) — no posted grant program,
  but board minutes show free technical assistance to towns on request:
  culvert replacement recommendations and cost estimates, drainage,
  stormwater outlets, NAACC culvert-assessment training. Their flood
  funding (SMIP/LFA) is Ashokan-watershed only.
- **Ulster County Legislature agendas** — county-level grant programs
  show up as resolutions before they are announced anywhere else.

## Looked at and rejected (2026-11-04)

- **SEVI Solar & EV Infrastructure grant** — $2.5M ARPA, up to $100k
  per municipality, 50% match; closed 2024-07-19. "Future rounds may be
  announced depending on funding availability." Watch
  participate.ulstercountyny.gov/solar-ev-grants.
- **Municipal Parks & Recreation grant** — $2M ARPA, one-off, awarded
  July 2023 to 16 municipalities (not the Town of Kingston). Closed.
- **Green the Scene** — 2025 was a grant (up to $2k, municipalities
  eligible); 2026 is technical support only and needs a 1,000-attendee
  or 5-food-vendor event. Not a grant.
- **Housing Action Fund Round 3** — due 2026-12-11, $2M, includes
  "housing-related infrastructure" up to $400k, but the page names
  developers, nonprofits and individuals as applicants. The NOFA PDF is
  the authoritative eligibility source and could not be read from here:
  ulstercountyny.gov/files/assets/countysite/v/3/planning/documents/housing/haf/round-3/uc-haf-round-3-nofa.pdf
  — someone should read it.
- **Catskill Watershed Corporation** — serves the West-of-Hudson NYC
  watershed; its Ulster communities are Olive, Shandaken, Denning and
  the hamlets around the Ashokan. The Town of Kingston is downstream of
  the dam and not in it, so CWC's many municipal programs don't apply.

## Quirks

- Announcements often go out by email to town supervisors and clerks
  rather than being posted. Ask to be on the distribution list; record
  what arrives in `grants/` with `source: ulster-county`.
- Several county pages (event-marketing fund, green-the-scene, the HAF
  NOFA) blocked automated reads. Read by hand.
