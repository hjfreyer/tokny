County-level pass-throughs and small programs. No single portal; this is
a list of offices to check.

## Where to look

- **Ulster County Planning Department** — periodically administers
  pass-through programs (CDBG-type small infrastructure, planning
  assistance, hazard mitigation plan updates). Check for RFPs and
  "municipal assistance" pages.
- **Ulster County Dept of Environment** — Climate Smart / green
  infrastructure support; sometimes small grants or technical help.
- **Ulster County Tourism / Economic Development** — occasional
  municipal marketing or event grants.
- **Ulster County Legislature agendas** — county-level grant programs
  show up as resolutions before they are announced anywhere else.
- **Ulster County Soil & Water Conservation District** — roadside
  ditch, culvert, and stream programs that fund highway-department-type
  work.

## Narrowing

Everything here is already scoped to Ulster County municipalities, so
the question is only fit and timing.

## Quirks

- Announcements often go out by email to town supervisors and clerks
  rather than being posted. Ask to be on the distribution list; record
  what arrives in `grants/` with `source: ulster-county`.
