Two families of programs, with different routes:

**Hazard mitigation** (BRIC, HMGP, Flood Mitigation Assistance) — the
state (DHSES) is the applicant and towns are sub-applicants. All three
require the town to have **adopted the current Ulster County Hazard
Mitigation Plan**; FMA also requires NFIP participation. Whether the
Town of Kingston has done either needs confirmation. BRIC was cancelled
in April 2025, reinstated by court order, ran one combined FY24–25
round that closed 2026-07-23, and has no announced successor.

**Fire grants** (AFG, SAFER, Fire Prevention & Safety) — the fire
department applies directly, annual window closing ~June. Only
relevant if the town operates the fire department rather than an
independent fire district; needs confirmation.

Rejected: EMPG (states only).

## Quirks

- fema.gov NOFO pages were not readable from here; details came from

  readable and carry eligibility and dates.
- DHSES's grants page (dhses.ny.gov/grants) is where NY sub-applicant
  windows are announced; "no open opportunities" as of 2026-11-04.
