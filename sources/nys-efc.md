Water and sewer money. Everything here presumes the town **owns water
or wastewater infrastructure** — whether it does needs confirmation; if
it doesn't, most of this file is moot.

## Programs filed

- Engineering Planning Grant (annual, June) — `2027-efc-engineering-planning-grant`.
  $50k/$100k; Mid-Hudson MHI cap ~$110k.
- Green Innovation Grant Program (annual, June) — `2027-efc-green-innovation-grant`.
  2026 awards were all ≥$450k.
- Green Resiliency Grant — open, due 2027-01-29, but $1M floor —
  `2027-efc-green-resiliency-grant`.
- WIIA / Intermunicipal Grant (annual, June–July) — `2027-efc-wiia-img`.
  50% hardship tier for systems serving ≤3,500 people.
- Clean Water for Housing (new Oct 2026, rolling, $50M rural set-aside,
  requires Pro-Housing certification) — `2026-efc-clean-water-for-housing`.

## Rejected

- Septic System Replacement Fund — homeowners apply through the county.
- Community Assistance Teams — free technical help, not a grant
  (518-402-7472). Worth a call before any EFC application.
